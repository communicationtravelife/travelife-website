Which GDSs is the Travelife software connected to?

Travelife's partners include the three major GDS (global distribution systems): Amadeus, Sabre and Galileo Travelport. The software also relies on airline aggregators such as Travelfusion, Misterfly and FlyConnect for scheduled and low-cost flights, as well as connections with airlines like Air Arabia, Lufthansa, flydubai, Hahn Air and easyJet. GDS / NDC connectivity is included in the Travelife ERP Connect plan, which includes 2 APIs, and in the Premium / Enterprise website offer.

How many airlines and accommodations can I access with Travelife?

Travelife gives access to more than 1,500 airlines and 500,000 accommodations. This volume comes from the hundreds of connections established since 2013 with hospitality and transport suppliers: GDS, aggregators, hotel hubs and ferry companies. Agencies see real-time availability and rates, then book from the same platform, without switching from one supplier extranet to another.

Which hotel wholesalers partner with Travelife?

Travelife's accommodation and activity partners include Hotelbeds, WebBeds, Jumbobeds, Jumbo Tours, GTA Travel, Restel, TravelClick, Expedia, MyHotels, TBO.com, RezLive and Viator. Thanks to these connections, Travelife brings together the inventory of multiple suppliers in a single flow: the agency compares rates, applies its margins and sells alongside its own hotel contracts. For sea transport, Travelife also works with GNV, Baleària and Trasmediterránea.

Which online payment solutions are compatible with Travelife?

Travelife's payment partners include CMI, Payzone, PayPal, Fatourati and Binga, allowing agencies to collect payment for their bookings online by local or international bank card. For customer communication, Travelife relies on Twilio and Sendinblue (SMS and email delivery). These integrations notably power the B2C website, which accepts full payment, an online deposit or a simple quote request.
