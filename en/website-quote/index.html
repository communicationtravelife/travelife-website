FAQ: travel agency websites

Features, GDS connections, SEO, timelines and GO SIYAHA funding: what you need to know before launching your project.

Why build a B2C website for my travel agency?

A B2C website lets your travel agency sell its travel products directly online, without relying solely on walk-in visits or phone calls.

With a professional website, your customers discover your offers and destinations, compare trips and send their booking requests at any time, 24/7.

Your agency also becomes more visible on Google and attracts new customers searching for trips online. In an increasingly competitive tourism market, it is now an essential tool to grow your business and your digital presence.

What features does a Travelife travel agency website include?

Websites built by Travelife are specifically designed to meet the needs of travel agencies and tour operators.

They include a travel search engine, booking modules for flights, hotels or tours, and secure payment: your customers book their trips directly online.

The website also includes an administration area (back office). There you manage your travel offers, publish content, track customer requests and view bookings. You get a complete, simple platform to run your tourism business.

Is the website connected to GDSs and travel suppliers?

Yes. The B2C website can be connected to various suppliers and GDS (global distribution systems) to automatically display availability for flights, hotels and other tourism services.

Thanks to API connections, your website accesses a wide catalog of offers from airlines, hotels and international distribution platforms.

The result: always up-to-date prices, automatic bookings and a wider catalog, without entering each offer by hand.

Is the website optimized for Google search?

Yes. Travelife websites are optimized for search engines (SEO), so your agency is more visible on Google and other search engines.

The site structure, optimized code, structured data (information read by Google) and dedicated destination pages help Google index your content properly.

You can also publish blog posts, travel guides or pages on specific destinations to attract truly interested visitors and strengthen your digital presence.

Does the website work on smartphones and tablets?

Yes. All websites built by Travelife are responsive, meaning they adapt automatically to every screen: computers, tablets and smartphones.

Today, a large share of travel searches happens on mobile. Your website therefore needs to be fast and easy to use on a phone.

A responsive website also ranks better on Google, and your customers browse your offers or book easily from their mobile.

How are booking requests handled?

Booking requests sent from your website are automatically recorded in your Travelife back office.

You track every request, send personalized quotes, turn requests into confirmed bookings and find all customer information in one place.

No more manual tracking in Excel or by email: you process travel requests much faster.

Travelife's digital solutions, such as building a B2C website connected to a booking system, can qualify under this program.

Through this scheme, agencies are supported and funded to modernize their digital tools, be more visible online and become more competitive. To check your eligibility, see our GO SIYAHA – Maroc PME page.

How long does it take to build a travel agency website?

Building a travel agency website is quite fast, because Travelife's solutions are designed specifically for tourism.

Depending on the level of customization, the features required and the integrations needed, your website can go live within a few weeks.

The Travelife team supports you throughout the project: website configuration, content integration, back-office training and go-live.

How much does a travel agency website cost with Travelife?

A Travelife travel agency website costs 99 € per month with the Starter plan or 399 € per month with the Advanced plan, plus a one-time setup fee (500 € for Starter, 1,500 € for Advanced).

The Premium / Enterprise plan, designed for large agencies and networks, is available on quote. On the Starter plan, an optional Launch Pack also lets us load your first 10 flagship tours (+ €300).

Travelife takes no commission on your online sales: the subscription is fixed, with no hidden fees. The full comparison is available on the B2C website pricing page.

What is the difference between the Travelife Starter, Advanced and Premium packages?

The difference mainly lies in API connectivity and support level. Travelife Starter suits agencies that sell their own products (direct hotel contracts, tours and activities, CMS, forms and blog), without external APIs, with standard support by email or ticket.

Travelife Advanced, the core target for outbound agencies and OTAs, adds 2 APIs (one hotel feed and one flight feed, excluding access fees charged by suppliers), the Packages module, setup of your pricing rules and priority support during business hours.

Premium / Enterprise offers unlimited access to the API hub (Amadeus GDS, Sabre GDS, Travelgate, multiple bed banks), dedicated servers and 24/7 VIP support.

What happens after I submit the quote form?

After you submit the form, a member of the Travelife team contacts you quickly to validate the chosen package and understand your business: own products, API needs, number of products, timelines.

You then receive your free, no-obligation quote. Once the project starts, Travelife handles domain setup, integration of your logo and brand guidelines, followed by training: 2 hours of online training for Starter, a 6 to 8-hour pack for Advanced.

You can also request a demo of the B2C website before committing, from the Contact us page.

Do I need WordPress or technical skills to manage my travel agency website?

No. The Travelife website is managed from the Travelife back office, without WordPress or any other external CMS, and without technical skills.

Three simple tools are all you need. The Theme Builder lets you choose the theme, colors, menu and footer, with a real-time preview. The Menu Builder organizes navigation. The Page Builder builds each page by adding modules (trips, search engines, forms, marketing content).

Built-in SEO tools let you set the titles, descriptions and content of each page to make your agency more visible on Google.

Is my travel agency website covered by a warranty and maintenance?

Yes. A complete website build by Travelife includes 1 year of warranty and maintenance.

Every Travelife website meets the technical standards of a professional site, with no hidden extras: responsive design for mobile and tablet, SEO optimization, HTTPS security and GDPR compliance.

Platform updates are automatic. Your data belongs to you and you can retrieve it at any time (full reversibility). Your website stays fast and secure, without your agency having to deal with the technical side.
