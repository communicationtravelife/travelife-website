How much does a travel agency website cost with Travelife?

Travelife offers three published plans. The Travelife Starter plan costs 99 € per month plus a 500 € setup fee: it suits agencies selling their own products (tours, direct hotel contracts, activities). The Travelife Advanced plan costs 399 € per month plus a 1,500 € setup fee, and includes 2 APIs (1 hotel feed, 1 flight feed), the Packages module and 6 to 8 hours of training. The Premium / Enterprise plan is quote-based. Travelife takes no commission on sales made through your website. Full details are on the B2C website pricing page.

How quickly can my travel agency website go live?

Allow about 2 weeks. Travelife is a turnkey solution designed specifically for travel agencies and tour operators: you don't have to write a specification or explain the travel business to a generalist provider. Our teams set up your domain name, integrate your logo and brand guidelines, then train you on the tool (see our support). The website comes with all the features needed to manage your offers, bookings and customers, and you can then add your trips and tours at your own pace from the back office.

Do I need WordPress or technical skills to manage my travel agency website?

No. The Travelife website is managed without an external CMS such as WordPress and without technical skills. The Theme Builder lets you choose the theme and change the colors, menu and footer, while seeing the result live. The Menu Builder organizes navigation, and the Page Builder builds each page by adding modules: trips, search engines, forms or marketing content. The trips, tours, photos and articles you enter are formatted automatically. Built-in SEO tools let you set the titles and descriptions of each page.

Which plan should I choose for my agency website: Starter, Advanced or Premium?

It depends on your business model. Travelife Starter is ideal for local experts and inbound agencies that showcase their own products without relying on external APIs. Travelife Advanced is for outbound agencies and OTAs that want to connect their suppliers and automate sales with flight and hotel API feeds. Premium / Enterprise is designed for large networks and tour operators: unlimited access to the API Hub (Amadeus GDS, Sabre GDS, Travelgate, multiple wholesalers), dedicated servers and 24/7 priority support. You can upgrade from one plan to another.
