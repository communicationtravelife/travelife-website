How does Travelife help me launch a high-performing B2C travel website?

Travelife provides a turnkey online sales website designed for individual customers. With our drag & drop editor, you build your showcase and e-commerce website without coding. The site handles your customer's entire journey: they search for an offer, compare it, then book and pay online securely.

What exactly does the setup fee cover?

The setup fee is paid once, at the start. It covers setting up your cloud space, creating your databases, configuring security and technical support during the launch of your website.

Does the pricing include commissions on my online sales?

No. Travelife wants to protect your profitability: we take no commission on sales made through your B2C website. You pay a fixed monthly subscription and know your budget in advance.

How much does a travel agency website cost with Travelife?

A travel agency website from Travelife costs 99 $ per month on the Starter plan (500 $ setup) and 399 $ per month on the Advanced plan (1,500 $ setup). The Premium / Enterprise plan, designed for large companies and agency networks, is available on quote. For the Starter plan, the highly recommended Turnkey Launch Pack adds the upload of your 10 flagship tours for 300 $. No commission is charged on your online sales.

What is the difference between the Starter, Advanced and Premium plans?

The Starter plan is for local agencies and DMCs that sell their own products, without external APIs: full CMS, direct hotel contracts, tours & activities, forms and blog, on a shared cloud server with standard support (email / ticket). The Advanced plan, the core offer for outbound agencies and OTAs, adds the Packages module, 2 included APIs (1 hotel + 1 flight), an optimized cloud server and priority support. The Premium / Enterprise plan offers unlimited access to API connectors, dedicated servers and 24/7 VIP support.

Can I easily upgrade my subscription plan?

Yes. You can start with your own local products (Starter plan), then move to a higher plan (Advanced or Enterprise) as soon as you want to add API connections or sell on new channels.

Can I sell both my own trips and third-party API products?

Yes, it is Travelife's main strength. You enter your own hotel, excursion and tour contracts (production engine) and, at the same time, display offers from global wholesalers connected by API (Hotelbeds, Travelgate, Amadeus, Air Arabia). Your customers put everything in a single cart.

Is the B2C website optimized for search engines (SEO)?

Yes. The website meets Google's speed criteria (Core Web Vitals). You control your pages' titles and descriptions (meta tags) as well as their addresses (URLs). A blog is also built in to attract visitors from Google on the keywords that matter to your agency.

How do margin and rate management (yield management) work?

You set your prices from a single back office. You define automatic margins (markups), as a percentage or a fixed amount, by API supplier, destination, product or season. You also choose whether booking fees are shown separately to the customer or included in the price.

What payment methods are available to end customers?

Travelife is connected to the main secure online payment solutions (local and international bank cards). You choose how to collect payment: full payment at booking, online deposit with automatic reminder for the balance, or a simple quote request.

Can I connect my travel agency website to Amadeus, Sabre or Hotelbeds?

Yes, from the Advanced plan. This plan includes 2 APIs of your choice (1 hotel feed and 1 flight feed, for example Air Arabia or Kyte), with full mapping and testing carried out by Travelife, excluding access fees (credentials) charged by suppliers. The Premium / Enterprise plan gives unlimited access to API connectors: GDS Amadeus, Sabre and Galileo, Travelgate, Hotelbeds, Webbeds, Jumbobeds, TBO and other consolidators. The Starter plan, meanwhile, is reserved for local inventory, without external APIs.

What training is included with my travel agency website?

Training depends on the plan. The Starter plan's Launch Pack includes 2 hours of online training, in addition to domain setup, logo integration and the basic brand guidelines. The Advanced plan includes initial training of 6 to 8 hours, as well as a custom setup of your pricing rules. Thanks to the drag & drop editor, your teams then create and edit their pages without coding. To go further, discover our onboarding and training.

Can I see an example of a travel agency website built with Travelife?

Yes. Travelife provides a B2C demo site so you can explore the booking journey exactly as your customers will see it, from searching offers to booking. You can also request a personalized demo via the website request form or by calling (+212) 5 21 50 06 05 to see how your own catalog and direct contracts would be presented.

Can I get my data back if I leave?

Yes. Your customer data, rate grids and booking histories belong to you. If you leave, you can export all of your databases in a structured format (full reversibility).

Are updates to the e-commerce solution included?

Yes. Travelife is a SaaS solution: security patches, maintenance of supplier API connections and new CMS features are installed automatically, with no interruption for your customers.
