Frequently asked questions about the Travelife AI Agent

Everything about the Travelife AI Agent: synchronization with Meta and WhatsApp, connection to your travel tools (TravelTech) and working alongside your team of advisors.

Does the AI Agent replace our travel advisors?

Absolutely not. The AI Agent was designed as a high-performance assistant to relieve your team of repetitive tasks. It handles the first welcome 24/7, precisely qualifies needs (budget, dates, family makeup, special wishes) and prepares the ground. As soon as the project takes shape, a human advisor takes over with all the keys to close the sale and add their human expertise.

What are the AI Agent's main day-to-day tasks?

The AI Agent acts as a true digital teammate for your agency:

- Immediate availability: answer travelers day and night, in under a minute.
- Rigorous qualification: ask the right questions so no important trip detail is missed.
- Dynamic search: query your catalogs and rates in real time.
- Follow-up and reminders: nudge prospects at the right time to turn more quotes into sales.

What is an AI agent for travel agencies?

An AI agent for travel agencies is a conversational assistant specialized in tourism, able to chat with travelers, understand their plans and take action in the agency's tools, within the limits of the permissions configured. The Travelife AI Agent is connected to the agency's offers and to Travelife SmartCRM: it greets the customer on WhatsApp or the website, qualifies the request (destination, dates, number of travelers, budget), searches available products, presents proposals with photos and prices, then passes a complete file to an advisor. The agency can thus handle requests received after closing time.

What is the difference between a traditional chatbot and the Travelife AI Agent?

A traditional chatbot follows fixed scripts and returns generic answers. The Travelife AI Agent understands natural language, including voice messages, and takes action: depending on the connections enabled, it queries your Travelife B2C catalog, your hotel rate sheets and your partner APIs to offer up-to-date availability and prices. It presents the booking terms set by the agency, attaches proofs of payment to the booking file and syncs everything to Travelife SmartCRM. It is an AI Agent built into your TravelTech and into our Travelife HUB vision, not a generic chatbot added to your website.

How does the handover from AI to a human advisor work?

As soon as a customer makes a complex request, asks to speak to a human or the AI detects a high-potential opportunity, the file is passed to the CRM. A notification is sent to the team with a full summary of the conversation. The traveler has nothing to repeat: the conversation continues without interruption.

On which channels can the AI Agent interact with customers?

The AI Agent is installed on your B2C website (as a modern, interactive chat bubble) as well as on WhatsApp, the essential channel where travelers like to chat quickly to plan their vacations or ask about their booking.

Does the Travelife AI Agent answer customers in multiple languages?

Yes. The Travelife AI Agent talks with travelers in several languages, such as French or Arabic, both in writing and through WhatsApp voice messages. It transcribes the message, detects the customer's intent (type of stay, destination, travel period, number of travelers) and structures this information in Travelife SmartCRM. Your advisor then gets a clear, actionable request, whatever language the traveler started in.

Can customers send voice messages or use natural language?

Yes, absolutely. Whether it's a detailed written message or a WhatsApp voice note, the AI Agent analyzes everyday language and the nuances of travelers' requests. It understands the context and automatically structures the information to feed your database.

How does the AI access our offers and rates in real time?

The AI Agent is directly linked to your travel tools (your TravelTech ecosystem): your Travelife B2C catalog, your hotel contract rates and your partner APIs. It doesn't give generic answers: it queries real databases to offer exact availability and up-to-date rates.

How does synchronization with Meta ads (Facebook Ads / Instagram Ads) work?

SmartCRM can be connected to the Meta ecosystem. When a prospect fills in a contact form (lead generation) on Facebook Ads or Instagram Ads, their contact details and travel information can be added automatically to your SmartCRM. Depending on configuration, the AI can also make a first contact by WhatsApp or SMS to qualify the prospect, before an advisor takes over.

How does synchronization with Travelife SmartCRM work?

Every interaction, qualified criterion, expressed preference and shared document is automatically centralized in Travelife SmartCRM. Your sales team finds the complete history and an AI-written summary: no more manual re-entry.

Can we connect our own suppliers and negotiated contracts?

Absolutely. Travelife is an open platform: you can add your own negotiated hotel contracts, partnership agreements and specific feeds. The AI Agent then uses them on its own to build its trip proposals.

Which payment and collection solutions are supported?

Travelife integrates with your usual payment collection solutions and secure payment gateways. The AI Agent can guide your customers and collect proof of payment; collection itself goes through the agency’s local and international gateways, such as CMI (ideal for the Moroccan market), Payzone, Stripe, PayPal, as well as other custom solutions depending on your setup.

How are deposits and receipts handled securely?

In line with your internal processes, the AI Agent presents the booking terms and can directly receive proofs of payment (such as a transfer order for a deposit). The document is immediately attached to the booking file in SmartCRM, so your finance team can validate it in no time.

What guarantees are there for data security and privacy?

The platform runs on a highly secure cloud infrastructure: encrypted exchanges (HTTPS), strict management of each team member's roles and access, and continuous automatic backups. Your customers' data is protected.

How long does it take to deploy the AI Agent for our agency?

Thanks to native integration with the Travelife ecosystem, setup is fast. Our experts configure the conversation scenarios to match your positioning, connect your channels (website and WhatsApp) and train your teams in just a few days (see our onboarding).
