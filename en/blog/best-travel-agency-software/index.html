What Is the Best Software for My Travel Agency?

Choosing good management software for a travel agency is a real challenge, and thanks to our experience with our many clients, we share…

TBy the Travelife team February 17, 2022 5 min read

In brief: the best software for a travel agency is the one that centralizes all its processes on a single platform. Before choosing, check eight criteria: cloud-based operation, user-friendly interface, integration with the major GDS, process automation, updates and security, invoicing management, report generation and an integrated CRM.

Whether you are a travel agency or a tour operator, whether you carry out most of your operations manually or use several software tools to run your business, the question arises: which software should you choose?

Choosing good software has become a real challenge today, especially as offers multiply by the day. The most important thing is for each agency to make the right choice: the one that will meet all its needs by centralizing all processes in one place, without redirection.

At Travelife, we have had the opportunity to support several travel agencies with our software built for travel businesses. This experience lets us share a few tips with you to avoid mistakes when choosing your software.

The 8 must-haves for choosing the right travel software

Cloud-based software

Today, many agents work from home. You should therefore choose cloud software, which lets the team connect to the system from any device, anywhere and at any time, with a simple internet connection.

A user-friendly interface

Good software means easy onboarding. It is therefore important to choose a solution that lets you customize your interface to suit your customers, while remaining user-friendly and simple to use.

Integration with the major GDS

Obviously, travel software must allow connection to the major GDS (Amadeus, Sabre, Galileo…). This integration automates your travel agency's processes, limits the risk of error and, above all, reduces the number of working hours.

Process automation

Among the key features to check is the automation of all your processes. You should be able to easily create, publish, manage and sell your products, which improves your efficiency and reduces the risk of error.

Updates and security

Several software products on the market offer no maintenance follow-up. Yet support and regular updates are essential to solve problems at any time and guarantee complete security for your customers' data.

Invoicing management

Invoicing is a central topic for travel agencies. This feature is therefore indispensable in the software you choose, with centralized, standardized documents (quotes, invoices, purchase orders, etc.). For Moroccan agencies, also check that it supports VAT on margin.

Report generation

Good travel software must be able to generate documents and, above all, relevant reports written in plain language. They let you track your agency's performance across every area: sales, expenses, products, customers, suppliers, employees, marketing…

Integrated CRM

With an industry in constant transformation, a diverse offering and increasingly demanding customers, agencies must choose software capable of managing customer relationships (CRM). The goal is to build stronger relationships with customers in order to retain them.

The right software: an all-in-one solution

Ultimately, good software is software that helps you better manage your travel agency, reduce the risk of error and the number of working hours, and increase your revenue: the all-in-one.

So take your time, compare the features of the available software and make the right choice for your travel software!

FAQ

How do you choose the best software for your travel agency?

Compare the features of the available solutions and favor an all-in-one tool that centralizes all your processes on a single platform. Check that it runs in the cloud, offers a user-friendly interface, connects to the major GDS, automates your processes, is maintained and secure, and includes invoicing, reports and CRM.

Why choose cloud software for a travel agency?

Many agents now work remotely. Cloud software lets the whole team connect to the system from any device, anywhere and at any time, with a simple internet connection. It also makes regular updates and maintenance, handled by the vendor, easier.

Why is GDS integration essential?

Connecting to the major GDS, such as Amadeus, Sabre or Galileo, lets you automate the agency's booking processes. It avoids re-entering data, limits the risk of error and significantly reduces the number of working hours spent on manual tasks.
