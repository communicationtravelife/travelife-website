FAQ

How much does Travelife travel agency software cost?

Travelife offers three monthly plans: CORE at €199 per month to digitize the back office (CRM, automatic invoicing, financial tracking), BUSINESS at €399 per month to manage your own inventory and build dynamic packages, and CONNECT at €599 per month for full API integration with global suppliers and real-time selling.

Are there setup fees?

Yes. Every installation requires an initial setup from €1,500. This amount covers tailored configuration of the software to your agency’s organization as well as expert training for your teams, so they are quickly operational on the modules included in the chosen plan.

Does Travelife take a commission on the agency’s sales?

No. Travelife’s model is a transparent subscription with no commission on sales. Whatever your booking volume, your revenue is 100% yours. Travelife positions itself as a technology partner, not as a business partner taking a share of your activity.

How many users are included in each plan?

Travelife plans include up to 5, 10 or 20 users right away, depending on the chosen plan. Unlike many solutions that charge for every additional license, you can equip your whole team without your subscription price rising with each new user.
