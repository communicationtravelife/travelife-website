What's at stake for Travel Tech in Morocco by 2030

On June 17, 2026, the DoubleTree by Hilton Casablanca hosted a major moment for the national tourism ecosystem. The National Meeting of…

TBy the Travelife team June 30, 2026 5 min read

In brief: on June 17, 2026, Travelife took part in the "Travel Tech: Made in Morocco" round table at the National Meeting of Tourist Transport Professionals, organized by the OMTT in Casablanca. Its CEO, Nabil Nouisser, presented three pillars to prepare Moroccan tourism for Vision 2030: intelligent automation, API connectivity and multichannel agility.

On June 17, 2026, the DoubleTree by Hilton Casablanca hosted a highlight for the national tourism ecosystem. The National Meeting of Tourist Transport Professionals, organized by the Moroccan Organization of Tourist Transport (OMTT), brought together the sector's key players for a shared reflection on the future of mobility and travel, on the eve of the major international milestones of 2030.

An honor and an opportunity for strategic exchange

For the Travelife team, taking part in this event was a true honor. In a context marked by positive momentum and a national drive to strengthen the competitiveness of Morocco as a destination, being invited to the "Travel Tech: Made in Morocco" round table reflects recognition of the driving role technology plays in transforming the sector.

The meeting helped strengthen ties between institutions, transport professionals and innovation players. It thus created an essential space for dialogue to prepare the Kingdom for the ambitions of Vision 2030.

Nabil Nouisser's address: technology serving Vision 2030

In his address, Nabil Nouisser, CEO of Travelife, stressed the imperative need to rethink digital infrastructure. He pointed out in particular that the transition to 2030 cannot happen without tighter control of data flows and smart centralization of services.

Nabil Nouisser placed particular emphasis on three strategic pillars:

  • Intelligent automation: to gain operational agility in the face of growing global tourism demand.
  • API connectivity: an essential lever for ensuring smooth pricing information and inventory management, and thus consistency across the entire distribution chain.
  • Multichannel agility: the importance of harmoniously orchestrating direct sales and international OTA platforms to maximize the visibility and economic performance of Moroccan players.

Towards a more competitive Moroccan tourism sector

The future of the sector, as outlined during the discussions, rests on an unprecedented convergence between traditional transport businesses and technological innovation. The exchanges clearly showed that a modern, high-performing sector directly helps strengthen Morocco's image as a safe, attractive and highly competitive destination.

Beyond modernizing fleets and physical infrastructure, it is the industry's ability to adopt digital tools that will determine the quality of tomorrow's visitor experience. By joining forces, professionals and technology experts are today charting the roadmap for a more agile, better-connected Morocco, ready to meet the challenges of the world's major events.

Note: Travelife is proud to bring its expertise in software engineering and digital distribution solutions, notably with its B2B booking platform, to support tourism professionals in their strategic ambitions.

We would like to thank the OMTT for this unifying initiative, which confirms that, through dialogue and collective action, the future of Moroccan tourism is being written right now.
