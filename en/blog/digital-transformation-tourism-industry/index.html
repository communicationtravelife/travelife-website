Digital Transformation Serving the Tourism Industry

The tourism industry has undergone major upheaval driven by digital technology, which has made automation…

TBy the Travelife team January 15, 2022 4 min read

In brief: digital transformation has upended the tourism industry: travelers compare prices, book at the last minute and post reviews online. For travel agencies, digital opens up new products, business models and markets, provided they automate their processes to become more efficient, something the health crisis has accelerated even further.

The travel industry has undergone a major upheaval driven by digital, which now makes automation more than necessary to meet the sector's new expectations. Tourists increasingly use digital tools to handle most of the steps involved in their stay, while enjoying a wide choice of offers. They can compare prices, book at the last minute and share their reviews on review sites.

The opportunities of digital transformation for tourism

Tourism is one of the sectors most strongly affected by digital change. It is no longer experienced as it used to be: thanks to e-tourism, m-tourism and social tourism, everything can now be done online. This digital transformation offers this industry several opportunities:

  • developing new products and services;
  • adopting new business models and processes;
  • gaining access to new markets;
  • improving position in global tourism value chains;
  • integrating into digital ecosystems.

Travel agencies and digital

Many travel agencies have understood the importance and, above all, the benefits of digital, because they need to improve the quality of their services, enrich the customer experience and, above all, optimize their resources. The role of travel agents in the digital age is evolving accordingly.

The health crisis has in fact had a positive effect on the digitalization of the tourism sector: it accelerated the transformation and forced players to adapt to new habits.

Speed, simplicity and a wide range of choices: these are some of the opportunities digital transformation has brought to the tourism sector. FAQ

What is digital transformation in tourism?

Digital transformation in tourism refers to the sector's shift to digital tools. Thanks to e-tourism, m-tourism and social tourism, travelers handle most of the steps involved in their stay online: they compare prices, book at the last minute and share their reviews. Professionals therefore need to automate their processes to meet these expectations.

What opportunities does digital offer travel agencies?

Digital makes it possible to develop new products and services, adopt new business models, access new markets, improve position in global tourism value chains and integrate into digital ecosystems. It also helps agencies improve service quality, the customer experience and the use of their resources.

What effect did the health crisis have on the digitalization of tourism?

The health crisis accelerated the digital transformation of the tourism sector. It forced travel agencies and other players to adapt quickly to travelers' new habits, as they favor speed, simplicity and a wide choice of offers available online.
