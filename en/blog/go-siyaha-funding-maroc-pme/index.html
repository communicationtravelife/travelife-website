GO SIYAHA funding: 531 projects approved to date

In brief: 531 projects have been approved under the GO SIYAHA program, including those of 136 companies for digitalization projects. Backed by 720 million dirhams, this Maroc PME and Ministry of Tourism program aims to support 1,700 tourism businesses by 2026. Since Travelife is listed by Maroc PME, travel agencies can have its purchase and implementation funded.

The Minister of Tourism, Handicrafts and Social and Solidarity Economy, Fatim-Zahra Ammor, announced on Monday in Rabat that 531 projects have been approved under the “Go Siyaha” program.

The GO SIYAHA program, initiated by Maroc PME and the Ministry of Tourism, is a key opportunity for Moroccan travel agencies looking to modernize their management and accelerate their digital transformation. With a budget of 720 million dirhams (MDH), this program aims to support 1,700 tourism businesses by 2026, offering them GO SIYAHA funding tailored to their digitalization and business management needs.

Travelife: a certified solution for GO SIYAHA funding

Travelife, business management software built for travel agencies, is officially listed by Maroc PME under the GO SIYAHA program. This listing allows agencies to benefit from GO SIYAHA funding and makes it easier to integrate Travelife into their business, with attractive financial support. To learn more, read our announcement: Travelife, software listed with Maroc PME.

A clear success: Travelife at the heart of GO SIYAHA funding

Since the program launched, 531 projects have been approved, including those of 136 companies for digitalization projects. A significant share of these companies chose Travelife as their management solution, confirming its leading position in the market for travel agency software.

The GO SIYAHA program is built around three main pillars:

  • Investment support, with grants of up to 35% for entertainment projects and 30% for classified tourist accommodation.
  • Advisory and technical support, covering up to 90% of technical expertise costs.
  • Sustainability promotion, including 40% investment aid for projects costing up to 10 MDH.

Travelife: the best way to take advantage of GO SIYAHA funding

Travel agencies that adopt Travelife enjoy several key benefits:

  • Direct access to GO SIYAHA funding thanks to the Maroc PME listing.
  • Coverage of up to 90% of the costs of acquiring and implementing the solution.
  • Optimized management: centralization of bookings, invoicing, accounting, VAT management and CRM.
  • Real-time synchronization with the GDS (Amadeus, Travelport, Sabre) for maximum efficiency.
  • Technical support to ensure a smooth, high-performing digital transition.

How can you benefit from GO SIYAHA funding with Travelife?

Travel agencies that want to take advantage of GO SIYAHA funding must submit their application to Maroc PME, presenting their Travelife implementation project. Our team is at your disposal to support you through these steps and ensure a fast, efficient process.

With Travelife, Moroccan agencies can modernize their management, optimize their operations and improve their competitiveness in the market. Travelife is also continuing its expansion in Africa, consolidating its position as the benchmark in travel agency digitalization.

FAQ

What is GO SIYAHA funding?

GO SIYAHA is a program launched by Maroc PME and the Ministry of Tourism, backed by 720 million dirhams. It aims to support 1,700 tourism businesses by 2026 around three pillars: investment support, advisory and technical support, and sustainability promotion. It notably funds digitalization and business management projects.

How many GO SIYAHA projects have been approved?

According to the announcement by Tourism Minister Fatim-Zahra Ammor in Rabat, 531 projects have been approved under the GO SIYAHA programme, including those of 136 companies for digitalization projects. A significant share of the companies concerned chose Travelife as the management solution for their travel agency.

What share of the cost of software can GO SIYAHA cover?

The consulting and technical support component of GO SIYAHA can cover up to 90% of technical expertise costs. For a travel agency, this can cover up to 90% of the costs of acquiring and implementing Travelife, software listed by Maroc PME. The application must be submitted to Maroc PME.
