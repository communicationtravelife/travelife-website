Tactics to Get More Bookings from My Travel Agency Website

Having an online presence and communicating on the Internet has become essential for every business, yet many spend large sums on…

TBy the Travelife team April 14, 2022 5 min read

In brief: to get more bookings, a travel agency website must offer simple, mobile-friendly navigation, high-quality photos and videos, an online booking system and a clear presentation of packages with calls to action. An active presence on the relevant social networks completes the picture.

Being present and communicating online has become unavoidable for every business. Yet many spend large sums on traditional communication and media, and neglect a profitable medium with minimal costs: the website.

Take the example of a travel agency: according to a recent online survey by Nielsen, nearly 71% of Moroccans prefer to book their trip online rather than in an agency. But having a website with a nice logo isn’t enough to increase conversion and get bookings. Travel agencies must therefore give great importance to the design of their website. Here are some tips to follow.

Make navigation simple and easy

It is very important to make navigation and the use of the features available on your site easy in order to offer users a smooth, effortless experience. Also think of phone users: a large share of customers make their bookings from their smartphone, which means you need to adapt your website for mobile use.

Use high-resolution photos and videos

Use attractive, very high-quality photos and videos that let potential customers discover your tours and imagine the experiences they want to have. Above all, create unique and attractive content so that your future customers know what to expect when they book with you. In other words: make your target audience want to travel!

Invest in an online booking system

Travelers now use the internet for the majority of their bookings, which makes an online booking system on your website essential. This system is not only valuable for the customer, but also for you: you are present on the market 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, wherever your customer is located. Travelife also offers travel agency website creation with an integrated booking engine.

Display your services and packages clearly

It is important to present your agency’s services well by describing them in a few sentences, with attractive wording, and adding visual elements such as a photo or video for each package.

Above all, don’t forget to place clear calls to action, such as “Book now!”, that let visitors book quickly and easily. Also make sure your customers can easily contact your advisors by providing basic contact information: phone number, email address and physical address.

Build a presence on all relevant social networks

Competition is already fierce with the travel offers of e-commerce sites. Travel agencies must therefore build a strong presence and well-targeted communication (optimized hashtags, organic and paid search…) to attract potential customers and turn them into travelers, then into advocates for your agency.

This presence can include Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Tripadvisor… Just remember to share quality content: promotions, past trips, new destinations, participation in fairs and events, contests. Generally, share visual content that encourages engagement and sharing, and makes your target audience want to travel! To go further, see our article on social media for your travel agency and our tips for promoting your travel agency website.

In conclusion

You must not miss out on a gold mine without tapping it, especially as more and more customers turn to the internet and online booking. It is therefore imperative to have a well-ranked and, above all, well-designed website (design and content) to grow your travel agency’s clientele by reaching a wide audience, for a low annual cost.

FAQ

How to get more bookings with your travel agency website?

Make navigation simple and mobile-friendly, use high-quality photos and videos, integrate an online booking system and present services and packages clearly with calls to action such as “Book now!”. Contact details should also be easy to find.

Why should a travel agency offer online booking?

Travelers now make the majority of their bookings online: according to a Nielsen survey, nearly 71% of Moroccans prefer to book their trip online. An online booking system also lets the agency be present on the market 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, wherever the customer is.

Which social networks should a travel agency use?

Relevant platforms include Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and Tripadvisor. The key is to share quality visual content there: promotions, past trips, new destinations, participation in fairs and events or contests, with optimized hashtags, in order to generate engagement and make people want to travel.
