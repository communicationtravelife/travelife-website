How to Restructure Your Travel Agency

For a few months now, tourism activity has been on an upward curve, and everything suggests that 2022 will be a good year, and well…

TBy the Travelife team July 26, 2022 4 min read

In brief: after the Covid-19 slump, the 2022 tourism recovery was the ideal time to restructure a travel agency. Four levers are proposed: sourcing new suppliers, testing new experience-focused products, putting digital tools in place and equipping yourself with management software with online booking available 24/7.

Border closures due to Covid-19 hit most industries hard, especially players in the tourism ecosystem, including travel agencies. The pandemic had a negative impact on their revenue: 80% of travel agencies saw a drop of 75% or more in revenue.

For a few months now, tourism activity has been on an upward curve again, and everything suggests 2022 will be a good year. It will therefore be the best time and the best opportunity to restructure your agency, favoring gentler, greener and more human forms of travel.

Here are our suggestions to help your travel agency find its place in the market in 2022:

Source new suppliers

Regularly consider renewing your suppliers, when it fits your business and what your customers are asking for. Stay up to date and keep track of the trending destinations of the moment.

Never be afraid to change suppliers: always think of your agency’s interests and, above all, your customers’ satisfaction. To go further, read our article on the importance of partnerships in the travel industry.

Test new products

Choosing a trip doesn’t happen in two minutes: travelers take time to think before choosing their next destinations. Agencies must therefore understand customer needs well, offer a unique, high-quality experience, and even anticipate future requests.

The most important thing is to give travel back its meaning and to stay attuned to new market trends, the ones that create unforgettable moments and bring back memories.

Stop selling trips, sell experiences!

Put digital tools in place

Tourism activity showed positive growth in 2022: it is the best time to rethink your travel agency and gain efficiency through a well-chosen digital strategy.

Competition is already fierce on travel offers. We therefore advise you to go digital and build a strong online presence, with well-targeted communication:

  • your website (SEO, design, content, etc.);
  • your social media (promotions, collaborations, new destinations, contests, etc.);
  • your Google My Business profile.

Take the time to upskill with new tools that will bring you real added value!

Use travel management software

It is important to choose software that helps you better manage your travel agency business, reduce the risk of errors and the number of working hours. Above all, it must offer an online booking system that keeps you present on the market 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, wherever the customer is located.
