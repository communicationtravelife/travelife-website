10 Blog Post Ideas for Your Travel Agency

The tourism sector is constantly evolving, and more and more travel agencies are turning to digital channels such as social media and websites…

TBy the Travelife team April 4, 2023 4 min read

In short: A travel blog helps a travel agency get noticed online, inform its visitors and make them want to travel. To write well, focus on practical tips, destination rankings, customer reviews, seasonal news and high-quality visuals; this article also offers 10 ready-to-use topic ideas.

The tourism sector is constantly evolving, and travel agencies are increasingly turning to digital channels, especially social media and websites, to reach their audiences. They share their trips there to attract new customers and improve their SEO, but unfortunately they often neglect the blog on their website.

Yet creating a travel blog is an excellent way to energize your travel agency. Still, it can be hard to find inspiration for articles, despite your knowledge of the services on offer and an experience that may exceed 10 years.

Travelers mainly use the Internet to find the trip that suits them. It is therefore important to also offer your visitors articles with practical information: they inform them while making them dream of a trip, which may later become a purchase from your agency.

Here are a few practices for writing your article:

  • Present the free and paid activities of a specific city.
  • Rank the best cities to visit around the world.
  • Write about a city or country and illustrate it with very high-quality photos.
  • Give advice and best practices to follow.
  • Let your customers share their experiences and give their opinions.
  • Describe the historical monuments of a specific city.
  • Follow the seasons and current events.
  • Use very high-quality images and videos.

A few examples of articles for your travel agency:

  1. The most popular destinations in Morocco
  2. The best destinations for family vacations
  3. The best travel ideas to recharge
  4. The most romantic destinations for couples
  5. The most affordable destinations in the world
  6. Tips for managing my travel budget well
  7. The best places to travel solo
  8. The best getaways for a complete change of scenery
  9. The best practical tips for preparing your trip
  10. The 5 best places to visit with your friends

FAQ

Why should a travel agency have a blog?

Travelers mainly search for their trips on the Internet. A travel blog lets the agency improve its SEO, offer practical information that informs its visitors, and make them dream of a trip they can then book with the agency. Many agencies favor social media and neglect their website blog, even though it is an excellent lever.

What topics should a travel agency blog cover?

Effective topics include: the most popular destinations in Morocco, the best destinations for family vacations, romantic destinations for couples, affordable destinations, tips for managing a travel budget, places to travel solo or with friends, and practical tips for preparing a trip.

How can I make a travel blog post more engaging?

Illustrate your articles with very high-quality photos and videos, present a city's free and paid activities, describe its historical monuments and offer destination rankings. Give practical advice, let your customers share their experiences and opinions, and follow the seasons and current events to publish content at the right time.
