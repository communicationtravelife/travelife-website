E-invoicing in Morocco 2026: Travelife already compliant with DGI standards

The Kingdom of Morocco is about to take a decisive step in modernizing its economic and tax ecosystem. Under the impetus of the Directorate…

TBy the Travelife team May 13, 2026 6 min read

In brief: the Directorate General of Taxes (DGI) is rolling out e-invoicing across Morocco from 2026, based on the international UBL standard in XML format. Two exchange modes are planned: EDI (automated, via API) and EFI (form-based entry, for very small and small businesses). Travelife software already supports these protocols, allowing travel agencies to approach the reform without disrupting their business.

The Kingdom of Morocco is about to take a decisive step in modernizing its economic and tax ecosystem. Driven by the Directorate General of Taxes (DGI), the introduction of e-invoicing is becoming the cornerstone of a global digitalization strategy. This structural reform is not just about digitizing documents: it deeply transforms the relationship between businesses and the administration.

For tourism professionals, anticipating this standard is essential to ensure the longevity of their business. With Travelife, your travel agency already has a visionary and compliant technology partner today.

A technical architecture built on international standards: UBL and XML

One of the major challenges of e-invoicing in Morocco in 2026 lies in standardizing data exchange. The DGI has adopted the international UBL (Universal Business Language) standard, based on the XML language, to ensure perfect interoperability between different information systems.

Unlike traditional invoices or simple PDFs, this structured format allows tax data to be read automatically and instantly. Travelife natively supports these complex protocols: our business management software automates the conversion of your sales into compliant XML files, so you can connect to future government platforms without technical difficulty. This software compliance is a strategic asset for avoiding any disruption to your business when the new regulation takes effect.

EDI and EFI: flexible solutions for every travel agency

The e-invoicing reform is meant to be inclusive and adapts to the structure of every Moroccan business. Travelife supports this diversity by offering tailored modules:

  • EDI mode (Electronic Data Interchange): aimed primarily at medium and large companies, this mode relies on full automation. Travelife uses robust APIs to transmit your invoices to the DGI in real time, which reduces processing times and the risk of human error.
  • EFI mode (Electronic Forms Interchange): for very small and small businesses, Travelife offers a simplified entry interface. This mode lets you meet your legal obligations with ease, without heavy technical infrastructure, while benefiting from the computing power of our system.

Security, legal archiving and data protection

Security is at the heart of e-invoicing. Retaining tax documents is no longer a mere storage option but a strict traceability obligation. Travelife guarantees full protection of your digital assets:

  • Secure digital archiving: your invoices are stored in a highly secure environment, in line with the legal retention periods set by the DGI.
  • Traceability and audit: every transaction generates a complete history, which makes tax inspections and your agency's internal audits easier.
  • Advanced cybersecurity: we apply the most demanding encryption standards to guarantee full confidentiality of your business exchanges and your customers' data.

The strategic benefits of making the switch with Travelife

Beyond strict e-invoicing compliance, adopting Travelife is a real lever for operational growth. By digitalizing your financial flows, you turn a regulatory constraint into a competitive advantage:

  • Optimized productivity: fewer manual administrative tasks let your advisors focus on what matters most: creating unique travel experiences for your customers.
  • Zero invoicing errors: automating the XML UBL format eliminates the risk of duplicates, omissions or calculation errors, and thus strengthens your agency's credibility with your partners.
  • Faster payment cycles: thanks to instant transmission and real-time validation, you optimize cash flow management and shorten collection times.
  • Transparency and modernity: an agency that masters e-invoicing projects an image of modernity and reliability, essential in today's tourism sector.

Conclusion: get ahead of the tax revolution today

The introduction of e-invoicing is a historic opportunity for Morocco's tourism sector to fully digitalize. By choosing Travelife, you do more than meet an obligation: you equip your agency with a high-performance, secure and future-oriented management tool. Don't let the reform catch you off guard, and prepare your digital transition with the travel industry expert in Morocco. FAQ

Which format has the DGI adopted for e-invoicing in Morocco?

The Directorate General of Taxes has adopted the international UBL (Universal Business Language) standard, based on the XML language. Unlike a simple PDF, this structured format allows tax data to be read automatically and instantly and ensures interoperability between companies' information systems and the administration's. Travelife automatically converts the agency's sales into compliant XML files.

What is the difference between EDI and EFI modes?

EDI mode (Electronic Data Interchange) relies on full automation: invoices are transmitted to the DGI in real time via API. It primarily targets medium and large companies. EFI mode (Electronic Forms Interchange) is aimed at very small and small businesses, with a simplified entry interface that lets them meet legal obligations without heavy technical infrastructure. Travelife offers modules for both modes.

How does Travelife archive electronic invoices?

Invoices are stored in a highly secure environment, in line with the legal retention periods set by the DGI. Every transaction generates a complete history that makes tax inspections and internal audits easier, and exchanges are protected by demanding encryption standards to guarantee the confidentiality of the agency's business data and customer data.
