Why choose Travelife over Odoo for a travel agency?

The tourism sector is a complex universe, with specific needs that generalist ERP solutions cannot always meet…

TBy the Travelife team June 11, 2025 6 min read

In brief: Odoo is a general-purpose ERP; for a travel agency, it must be heavily customized to handle allotments, industry APIs (Amadeus, Hotelbeds, channel managers) and the VAT on margin under Article 125 in Morocco, which is costly and risky. Travelife Manage was built from scratch for these needs and integrates them natively, as a turnkey solution maintained by tourism experts.

Tourism is a complex world, with specific needs that general-purpose ERP solutions cannot always meet. Today, many agencies are considering Odoo or other open-source platforms to run their business. Yet this approach can quickly show its limits. Here is why.

1. Travel is a profession in its own right

Unlike more traditional industries, travel agencies must manage:

  • a wide variety of products (flights, hotels, tours, activities, packages, transfers);
  • specific constraints (allotment management, real-time bookings, quotas, complex pricing);
  • very precise local accounting rules (such as Article 125 in Morocco on VAT on margin);
  • complex supplier-customer relationships, with strict traceability.

Odoo, however powerful, is designed as a generic ERP. Its core does not take all these industry specifics into account. The platform therefore has to be heavily developed and adapted, which is costly and risky.

2. The challenges of integrating industry APIs into Odoo for travel agencies

Integrating APIs such as Amadeus (ticketing), Hotelbeds (hotels) and channel managers is not just a technical matter. These APIs demand fine-grained management, fast synchronization, strong robustness and very precise business rules.

Developing these integrations in a non-specialized ERP takes considerable resources, entails significant costs and carries a high risk of malfunctions. At Travelife Manage, we built these integrations natively with a strong focus on the travel business, which guarantees stability, speed and accuracy.

3. Accounting specific to the tourism industry

Moroccan taxation, notably the application of Article 125 (VAT on margin), is a particularity you absolutely must master. Odoo does not include this logic by default: it has to be developed as an overlay, which complicates maintenance and updates.

Travelife Manage natively includes these rules, allowing agencies to stay compliant with no extra effort. To learn more, see our dedicated page on VAT on margin software for travel agencies.

4. User experience and tailored workflows

Travel agency workflows are highly specific: managing client booking files, approvals, payment tracking, voucher issuance, and handling credit and unpaid balances. Open-source solutions often require heavy interface customization to come close.

Travelife Manage is designed from the ground up to meet agencies’ day-to-day business needs, which streamlines daily work and boosts productivity.

5. Support, maintenance and evolution

A heavily modified open-source ERP can become hard to maintain and evolve, and leave you dependent on a team of developers who are often far removed from your business.

Travelife Manage is a turnkey solution, maintained and enhanced by a team of tourism experts who understand your needs perfectly.

6. The Travelife story: why building from scratch was essential

Before launching Travelife Manage, we tried adapting open-source solutions like Odoo, and even a more powerful information system like SAP Business One, for a travel agency. We quickly realized that, despite their flexibility, these platforms did not fully meet the sector’s very specific requirements.

The travel industry, with its complex allotment management, multiple API integrations, local tax rules and diverse product range, requires a system designed for these constraints from the start.

So we made the strategic choice to start over from zero and build Travelife Manage from scratch. This approach allowed us to:

  • fully master every business mechanism, with no compromise;
  • natively integrate all the essential APIs, with robustness and speed;
  • precisely adapt the solution to Moroccan accounting rules, notably Article 125 on VAT on margin;
  • deliver a smooth user experience and a workflow 100% dedicated to agencies, covering inbound, outbound, corporate, B2B and B2C.

Today, this choice lets us offer a stable, scalable product perfectly aligned with the real needs of travel agencies.

Travelife Manage or Odoo: which should you choose?

Choosing Travelife Manage means opting for a dedicated, reliable, compliant and scalable industry solution, designed to support every travel agency in its specific needs. Conversely, choosing Odoo or a generic open-source ERP often means accepting significant customization costs, functional risks and added complexity.

FAQ

Is Odoo suitable for a travel agency?

Odoo is a powerful generic ERP, but its core does not natively handle travel-specific requirements: allotments, quotas, real-time bookings, complex pricing, GDS integration and VAT on margin. To use it in an agency, it must be extensively developed and adapted, which generates significant costs, risks of malfunction and more complex maintenance during updates.

Does Odoo handle Article 125 VAT on margin in Morocco?

No, not by default. The VAT on margin logic provided for by Article 125 must be developed as an overlay in Odoo, which complicates maintenance and updates. Travelife Manage natively includes these Moroccan accounting rules, allowing travel agencies to stay compliant without custom development or extra effort.

Why was Travelife built from scratch?

The Travelife team first tried to adapt Odoo, then SAP Business One, for a travel agency, without managing to cover all the sector’s requirements. It therefore chose to start over from zero in order to master every business mechanism, natively integrate the essential APIs, comply with Moroccan taxation and offer a dedicated workflow for inbound, outbound, corporate, B2B and B2C.
