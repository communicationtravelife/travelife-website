Travelife news: CRM pipelines for travel agencies

Travelife news: the CRM pipelines revolutionizing travel agency management. Travelife, commercial management software dedicated to travel agencies…

TBy the Travelife team January 19, 2026 6 min read

In brief: the Travelife CRM, a business management software for travel agencies, now includes pipelines: a Tickets / after-sales pipeline for customer service and a sales pipeline for tracking deals. These stage-based boards give a clear, real-time view of every booking file, ticket and opportunity.

Travelife, business management software built for travel agencies, keeps evolving to meet the demands of an ever more competitive market. Already recognized for its rich CRM features, Travelife now introduces a major new feature: pipelines.

This evolution marks an important turning point in managing customer service (tickets / after-sales) and sales tracking. It provides a clear, structured and collaborative view of the agency's key processes.

Recap of the CRM features already available in Travelife

Before pipelines arrived, the Travelife CRM already offered a complete functional foundation perfectly suited to the needs of travel agencies.

Centralized management of customers and suppliers

Travelife lets you manage customers and suppliers through detailed records that centralize all the essential information: contact details, communication history, documents, sales and payments. This centralization makes follow-up easier and improves the quality of the customer relationship.

Smart duplicate management

The CRM includes a duplicate detection and management system that guarantees a clean, reliable and usable database. Agencies thus avoid errors, lost information and inconsistencies.

Collaborative alert management

Alerts are a true internal coordination tool. They can be created, scheduled and assigned to colleagues within the same agency, ensuring better follow-up of tasks and commitments.

Agent tracking and sales performance

Managers get clear indicators to analyze their agents' activity: sales made, payments collected and margins generated. This visibility enables precise steering of the agency's overall performance.

Major new feature: CRM pipelines in Travelife

With the introduction of pipelines, Travelife strengthens its CRM by adding a visual and operational dimension to process management. Pipelines organize information as stage-based boards that give an instant read on how each booking file is progressing.

Tickets / after-sales pipeline: structured customer service management

The Tickets / after-sales pipeline is designed to efficiently manage customer requests, complaints, after-sales follow-up and internal alerts.

How the Tickets / after-sales pipeline works

Tickets are organized by clear statuses, such as:

  • New;
  • In progress;
  • On hold;
  • Resolved.

Each ticket can be:

  • linked to a customer or a supplier;
  • assigned to a team member;
  • prioritized;
  • tracked using key dates (start date, end date, SLA deadline).

Benefits for the agency

This system provides:

  • a global, real-time view of after-sales service;
  • better distribution of tasks;
  • improved compliance with deadlines;
  • a significant improvement in customer satisfaction.

Teams gain in responsiveness, efficiency and organization.

Sales pipeline: mastering the sales cycle

The Travelife sales pipeline is dedicated to tracking opportunities and managing the agency's sales activity.

How the sales pipeline is organized

Opportunities are distributed across the stages of the sales cycle, for example:

  • New lead;
  • Qualification;
  • Proposal sent;
  • Negotiation.

Each opportunity moves visually from one stage to the next, so sales teams know exactly where each sale stands.

Benefits for sales teams

The sales pipeline enables:

  • better work organization;
  • clear tracking of prospects and opportunities;
  • forecasting of future sales;
  • an increase in the conversion rate.

Why pipelines are a strategic asset for travel agencies

Thanks to pipelines, Travelife offers:

  • clear visibility across all processes;
  • stronger collaboration between teams;
  • significant time savings in day-to-day management;
  • a smoother, more professional customer experience.

These features turn the CRM into a true operational and sales management tool.

Closing

With the integration of the Tickets / after-sales pipelines and the sales pipeline, Travelife confirms its position as an innovative CRM specialized in travel agencies. These new features help structure operations, optimize sales performance and durably improve the quality of customer service.

Travelife thus establishes itself as a strategic partner supporting the growth and digitalization of travel agencies. To go further in automating customer relations, also discover the Travelife SmartCRM AI agent.

FAQ

What is a pipeline in the Travelife CRM?

A pipeline is a stage-based board that shows how the agency's files are progressing. In Travelife, there is a Tickets / after-sales pipeline for customer service and a sales pipeline for sales. Each item moves visually from one stage to the next, giving teams an instant read on their activity and making collaboration easier.

How does the Tickets / after-sales pipeline work?

Tickets are sorted by status: new, in progress, on hold and resolved. Each ticket can be linked to a customer or a supplier, assigned to a team member, prioritized and tracked with key dates, including the SLA deadline. The agency thus gets a global, real-time view of its after-sales service and meets deadlines more reliably.

What stages does the sales pipeline include?

The sales pipeline distributes opportunities across the stages of the sales cycle, for example new lead, qualification, proposal sent and negotiation. Teams know exactly where each sale stands, clearly track their prospects, anticipate future sales and can thus improve their conversion rate.

Which CRM features already existed in Travelife?

Before pipelines, the Travelife CRM already offered customer and supplier records centralizing contact details, communications, documents, sales and payments, duplicate detection, collaborative alerts that can be scheduled and assigned to team members, and agent tracking by sales, payments collected and margins generated.
