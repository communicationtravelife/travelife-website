Social Media for Your Travel Agency

Social media has become more essential than ever for businesses, and this is also true for travel agencies since…

TBy the Travelife team March 7, 2022 5 min read

In brief: social media has become an essential tool for travel agencies: it lets them create content, build awareness and retain customers. To succeed, an agency must be present on the right networks (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn…), post valuable content regularly and rely on creativity: contests, customer testimonials and influencer collaborations.

Social media has become more than ever an essential tool for businesses, and this is also true for travel agencies, which already face heavy competition from the many travel offers appearing on the market every day.

Generally speaking, social media communication has transformed the way travel agencies present their services to customers and prospects, making travel decisions more convenient. Travel professionals increasingly use digital channels, and social media in particular, to create content, build awareness and above all retain their customers.

Social media, an essential tool for travel agencies

Digital is a great help for travel agencies’ visibility, and social media communication is just as valuable thanks to sharing and sponsored posts, which help promote your business and retain travelers.

Indeed, no one can deny that the traveler journey begins and ends online, and above all on social media. Supporting travelers throughout their experience has become essential, as it shapes satisfaction and the continuity of the relationship with the traveler, particularly online.

Which social networks should a travel agency be on?

Choosing the right social networks is undoubtedly a path to success for a travel agency: it helps nurture and grow the relationship with followers in order to build trust. Every agency should therefore be present first and foremost on certain networks, namely: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn… Other channels such as TikTok for travel agencies are also gaining ground.

What content should you post on your agency’s social media?

Social media communication for travel agencies is not limited to sharing trips. It is rather about regularly feeding your accounts with quality content: not only photos, but also content that adds value for followers, along with attractive offers.

Be creative! Also consider:

  • running contests and events;
  • sharing memories or testimonials from your customers;
  • creating digital collaborations with influencers…

Your posts also work better when they point to a travel agency website where your followers can browse and book your offers.

Always seek to get closer to your audience, polish your social media communication and personalize your customer relationships: this will help you improve your brand’s visibility, retain your customers and above all achieve a better return on investment.

FAQ

Why should a travel agency be on social media?

Because the traveler journey now begins and ends online, especially on social media. Being present lets the agency create content, build awareness against heavy competition, promote its offers through sharing and sponsored posts, and above all retain its customers by building a relationship of trust with its followers.

Which social networks should a travel agency prioritize?

A travel agency should be present first and foremost on Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn, networks that help nurture and grow the relationship with followers. Being present is not enough: the agency must regularly feed its accounts with quality content and attractive offers in order to build a lasting relationship of trust with its community.

What type of content should a travel agency post?

Beyond destination photos, the agency should publish content that adds value for its followers and attractive offers. Contests, events, customer memories and testimonials, and influencer collaborations are good levers for gaining visibility, retaining customers and improving return on investment.
