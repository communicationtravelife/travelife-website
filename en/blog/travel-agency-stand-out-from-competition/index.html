How to stand out from the competition: tips for travel agencies

The tourism sector is an extremely competitive market where travel agencies compete to attract and retain as many…

TBy the Travelife team July 4, 2023 4 min read

In brief: facing online booking platforms, a travel agency stands out from the competition through the quality of its service and welcome, tailored and exclusive offers, and active customer loyalty efforts. This article presents 16 practical tips, from the agency atmosphere to the loyalty program, including social media and innovative technologies.

The tourism sector is an extremely competitive market, where travel agencies compete to attract and retain as many customers as possible. With the emergence of digital technologies, fierce competition has set in, especially between traditional travel agencies and online booking platforms.

Travel agencies must therefore adapt: offer travelers a multitude of options for organizing their trip, while providing quality services to stay attractive and stand out from their competitors.

Many tips help travel agencies stand out and attract more customers. In this article, we share methods that can help a travel agency stand out, whatever strategy it follows.

Here are 16 tips to stand out from the competition

  1. Create a warm atmosphere in your agency.
  2. Offer exceptional customer service.
  3. Build partnerships to offer unique experiences.
  4. Offer attractive promotions.
  5. Offer concierge services to customers.
  6. Use social media to promote your business.
  7. Create tailor-made packages.
  8. Offer personalized guides for each destination.
  9. Offer sustainable, eco-responsible travel options.
  10. Organize exclusive events and experiences for loyal customers.
  11. Use innovative technologies to improve the customer experience.
  12. Offer discounts for group bookings and loyal customers.
  13. Offer comprehensive travel insurance for customers’ peace of mind.
  14. Offer honeymoon packages.
  15. Create video travel guides.
  16. Offer a loyalty program to regular customers.

In summary

There are several ways for a travel agency to stand out from the competition. The tourism sector is strongly influenced by digital, which has made the customer experience easier but has also made competition fierce with the rise of online booking platforms. That is why every agency must adapt and redouble its efforts to stand out.

FAQ

How can a travel agency stand out from the competition?

A travel agency stands out first through exceptional customer service and a warm welcome. It can also offer tailor-made packages, personalized guides, exclusive experiences through partnerships, sustainable travel options and a loyalty program. Social media and innovative technologies complete these levers to improve the customer experience.

Why is competition so strong between travel agencies?

Digital has profoundly transformed tourism. Online booking platforms let travelers organize their own trips, which creates fierce competition with traditional agencies. To stay attractive, agencies must offer a wide choice of options and quality services that platforms don’t provide.

How to build customer loyalty for a travel agency?

Several tips aim directly at loyalty: organizing exclusive events and experiences for loyal customers, granting them discounts, especially on group bookings, and setting up a loyalty program for regular customers. Exceptional customer service remains the foundation of any lasting relationship.
