Travelife wins at the African Excellence Awards 2025

We are proud to announce that Travelife has been honored by the prestigious British media outlet MEA Markets, as part of the African Excellence Awards 2025, in…

TBy the Travelife team July 17, 2025 4 min read

In short: In 2025, Travelife received the title of “Best Travel Agency Software Company – Morocco” at the African Excellence Awards, presented by the British media outlet MEA Markets. The award recognizes its 100% cloud management software for travel agencies and its contribution to the digital transformation of tourism in Morocco and Africa.

We are proud to announce that Travelife has been honored by the prestigious British media outlet MEA Markets at the African Excellence Awards 2025, receiving the title of “Best Travel Agency Software Company – Morocco”.

This international distinction reflects the excellence of our software solution dedicated to travel agencies, as well as our contribution to the digital transformation of the tourism sector in Morocco and Africa.

Prestigious recognition in a showcase of African excellence

The African Excellence Awards program, organized each year by MEA Markets, highlights the companies, professionals and organizations shaping the continent’s economy through innovation, quality of service and performance.

Winners are selected through a rigorous multi-stage process: nomination, qualitative assessment, impact study, reputation check and final validation by an independent editorial committee.

The award presented to Travelife in the “Best Travel Agency Software Company – Morocco” category highlights not only our technical expertise but also our commitment to travel professionals. We are especially honored to have been singled out among so many dynamic and innovative players.

A solution designed for today’s travel agencies

Since its beginnings, Travelife has been developing practical tools to meet travel agencies’ daily needs. Our management software centralizes and simplifies all operations: bookings, invoicing, customer follow-up, reporting, and much more.

Our strength lies in the combination of technology and human support. We offer an intuitive, 100% cloud platform accessible from anywhere, along with responsive customer service to support agencies in their digital transformation.

Thanks to this approach, we have earned the trust of many agencies in Morocco and in several African countries. This award confirms our position as the go-to solution for travel professionals.

A rigorous selection by MEA Markets

The African Excellence Awards aim to identify and reward the top-performing companies, initiatives and leaders across various sectors throughout the African continent. Selection is based on several criteria: service excellence, innovation, economic impact, customer satisfaction and sustainability of results.

Each winner undergoes an in-depth assessment, from nomination through to final validation by an independent editorial panel.
