Key facts Manage, Travelife’s travel agency management system

  • DefinitionTravelife Manage is a 100% cloud commercial and operational management system, developed in Casablanca for travel agencies and tour operators.
  • WorkflowTravelife Manage covers 9 steps in a single tool: login, booking, quote, order, invoice and voucher, client payment, supplier purchase, reports and VAT.
  • BookingsTravelife Manage combines connected bookings (GDS and hotel APIs) and offline bookings (direct contracts, allotments, packages).

1. What is Travelife Manage?

Travelife Manage is cloud ERP software dedicated to travel agencies and tour operators. It brings all commercial, operational and financial operations together in a single platform, to simplify the agency's day-to-day management.

Its interface is easy to pick up and evolves with your needs: your teams manage bookings, clients, payments and suppliers from a single tool.

2. What features does Travelife Manage offer?

Travelife Manage offers a complete suite of features for travel agencies: booking and client file management, advanced CRM with interaction history, automatic generation of quotes, invoices and vouchers, payment management and tracking, real-time dashboards and reporting.

With these features, you stay in control of your agency's entire commercial activity.

3. Can Travelife connect to GDSs and supplier APIs?

Yes. Travelife connects to various GDS (Amadeus, Sabre, Travelport) and tourism suppliers via secure APIs. You thus access a wide range of flights, hotels, activities and tourism services, directly from the platform.

Bookings are copied automatically into your management system: no double entry. Acronyms such as GDS, NDC or BSP are explained in our travel agency glossary.

4. Can the software handle offline bookings?

Yes. Travelife handles both API-connected bookings and offline bookings. You can integrate your direct hotel contracts, air allotments or custom packages.

This flexibility lets you manage every type of travel product in a single tool.

5. How does Travelife improve an agency’s sales management?

Travelife automates the steps of the sale: creating the quote, converting it into an order, invoicing and payment tracking. Continuously updated dashboards also show performance by product, by sales channel or by salesperson.

Agencies can thus make strategic decisions based on reliable, up-to-date figures.

6. Is Travelife compatible with B2B and B2C platforms?

Yes. Travelife Manage is the heart of your digital ecosystem: it brings together bookings from your B2B platform, your B2C website and your direct in-agency sales.

All bookings then follow a unified workflow: validation, invoicing, payment and commission tracking.

7. How can I get a demo of the Travelife software?

To discover all our features, you can request a personalized demo via our contact form. Our team studies your business and shows you concretely how the software simplifies the management of your travel agency.

The demo is free and shows you in concrete terms how to digitalize your operations with Travelife.

8. What are the steps of a travel booking file in Travelife Manage?

A file goes through 9 steps in Travelife Manage, without switching tools: the agent logs in with a secure profile, makes the booking (GDS, hotel API or offline catalog), creates a multi-line quote or proforma with automatic margin calculation, then converts it into a firm order as soon as the client accepts.

They then extract the invoice and voucher, record the client payment (deposits, receipts, credit notes), track the supplier purchase and its due dates, check cash reports and bank reconciliation, and let the software calculate VAT on margin (see our VAT on margin software).

9. Does Travelife Manage generate vouchers and rooming lists?

Yes. Once the quote is accepted, Travelife Manage automatically generates the customer invoice, issues vouchers for service providers and lets you extract and export the booking file’s rooming list.

Passenger information is entered only once, including for groups: it is carried over into all documents. You save time on complex files such as tours or Umrah programs.

10. How much does the Travelife Manage travel agency management system cost?

Travelife Manage costs from $199 per month (ERP Core plan, 5 users included, $1,500 setup fee). The ERP Business plan at $399 / month adds the administration console, packages, hotel contracts, allotments and rooming list; the ERP Connect plan at $599 / month adds the booking engine, GDS / NDC connection and 2 supplier APIs.

No commission is taken on your sales. See the detailed pricing.

11. Can Travelife Manage be used remotely or on a tablet?

Yes. Travelife Manage is a 100% cloud solution accessible from a web browser, on a desktop computer, laptop or tablet. Your teams can work from the agency, from home or on the move with the same tool and the same real-time data.

There is no installation or backup to manage: if a computer breaks down, you simply log in again from another device. Updates are regular and technical support is included.
