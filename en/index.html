Accounting & VAT on margin

Why choose Travelife?

Travelife is all-in-one management software for travel agencies, tour operators and tourism professionals. This 100% cloud ERP brings all your operations together on a single platform: bookings, B2B/B2C sales, invoicing, accounting, CRM and payment tracking.

It connects to GDS and to many suppliers via API. Tasks flow automatically, errors decrease and your teams become more productive.

Who can use Travelife?

Our solution is designed for travel agencies, tour operators, DMCs / inbound agencies, agency networks, OTAs and self-employed professionals in the tourism sector. Thanks to its cloud architecture and flexible modules, Travelife adapts equally well to small businesses and to multi-agency organizations operating internationally.

What services does Travelife offer?

Our solution offers a complete package to manage every activity of a travel agency. The platform includes an ERP for sales and operations management, a smart, connected CRM (Travelife Smart CRM), an invoicing and accounting module, a payment and cash-flow tracking system, as well as a B2B platform and a connected B2C website.

The platform also connects to the leading GDSs (Amadeus, Sabre, Travelport…) and to suppliers via API for online booking of flights, hotels and transfers. It also handles offline bookings such as contracted hotels, visas, activities and tailor-made tours, all centralized in a single system.

How long has Travelife been around and how many agencies use it?

Travelife was founded in 2013 in Casablanca. Since then, the solution has become one of the leading technology platforms for travel professionals in Morocco: more than 200 travel agencies now trust it, in Morocco and abroad. To learn more, discover who Travelife is.

The software was recognized at the World Summit Awards (best digital solution in Morocco) and received the "Best Travel Agency Software Company – Morocco" award at the African Excellence Awards 2025. Travelife is also listed with Maroc PME under the GO SIYAHA program.

What is the difference between Travelife and a generic ERP like Odoo for a travel agency?

Travelife is 100% industry-specific software, designed from the start for travel agencies, whereas a general-purpose ERP has to be adapted and configured for this sector. Travelife natively includes what an agency needs: multi-service travel files (flights, hotels, transfers, visas, tours), connections to GDS and suppliers via API, vouchers and rooming lists, commission management and automatic calculation of the VAT on margin specific to travel agencies.

Your teams work in a tool that speaks their language, with no custom development. To learn more, read our article Travelife or Odoo for a travel agency.

Is Travelife suitable for travel agencies in Morocco?

Yes. Our software was designed with the specific features of the Moroccan market and local tourism sector in mind. The platform includes VAT on margin management, compliant invoicing, payment tracking and supplier management.

Thanks to its cloud architecture, Moroccan agencies can centralize their sales, automate their sales processes and improve the profitability of their operations.

Is Travelife suitable for international agencies?

Absolutely. Travelife is a cloud ERP solution accessible anywhere in the world. The platform supports multi-currency, multi-agency and multi-user management, making it ideal for agency networks and tour operators operating in several markets.

API and GDS integrations provide access to a wide inventory of flights, hotels and travel services from a single interface.

Is Travelife suitable for small agencies or large companies?

Travelife is a scalable solution designed for micro-businesses, SMEs and mid-sized travel agencies, as well as large and very large companies. A small agency can start with the essential modules and gradually add new features as its business grows.

Larger organizations can manage several agencies, teams and sales channels from a centralized platform.

Do I need to install software on the agency’s computers to use Travelife?

No. Travelife is 100% cloud software (SaaS): it is used directly from a web browser, on a desktop computer, laptop or tablet, from the agency or from home.

There is no server to buy and no backup to manage: if a computer breaks down, you simply log in again from another device and find all your data. Updates are deployed automatically by Travelife, with no action required on your part.

Does Travelife include a CRM to manage customers?

Yes. The solution includes a complete CRM built for travel agencies. The platform centralizes customer information, booking history, payments, travel preferences and communications.

With this tool, agencies can better track their prospects, segment their database and run targeted sales campaigns to improve loyalty and grow revenue.

What is Travelife Smart CRM and what does it offer?

Travelife Smart CRM is our new-generation customer relationship management module. Combined with smart assistants (AI), it gathers your travelers' full history and sends follow-ups automatically.

It sorts and qualifies inquiries received on your channels (WhatsApp, your website...) 24/7. Your advisors see everything on one screen: they convert inquiries into sales faster and build lasting client loyalty.

How does Smart CRM handle WhatsApp and multichannel interactions?

Thanks to Travelife Smart CRM’s deep integration, messages received via WhatsApp or your website chat are instantly analyzed and qualified by AI.

Inquiries received at night or on weekends are handled in real time. A complete file is created in the CRM and automatically assigned to one of your advisors.

Can I manage flights, hotels and other services?

Yes. The platform has an advanced module for managing flights, hotels, transfers, activities and other travel services from a single interface.

Bookings can be connected via API or GDS, or created manually for custom packages or specific supplier contracts.

Does Travelife automatically calculate VAT on margin for travel agencies?

Yes. Travelife applies the VAT rules specific to travel agencies and automatically calculates VAT on margin from each booking file: selling price, supplier costs and margin are known from the moment of booking.

Every invoice issued is recorded immediately in the accounting module: no more manual calculations, and your tax compliance is ensured. Your accountant views this data in real time, without traveling, and closes accounting periods faster. How it works is detailed on our VAT on margin software for travel agencies page.

How much does Travelife travel agency software cost?

Travelife costs from €199 per month. The subscription comes in three plans: Travelife ERP Core at €199 / month (5 users included, €1,500 setup fee), Travelife ERP Business at €399 / month (10 users, €3,000 setup) and Travelife ERP Connect at €599 / month (20 users, €4,000 setup), which adds the booking engine, GDS / NDC connection and 2 supplier APIs included.

In Morocco, these costs can be funded up to 90% by the GO SIYAHA program. Details are available on the Travelife software pricing page.

Does Travelife take a commission on the agency’s sales?

No. Travelife takes no commission on sales made by your agency, whether in-store, on your B2C website or via your B2B platform. You only pay a fixed monthly subscription, giving you full control over your budget, whatever your business volume.

You can switch plans at any time so your tools stay aligned with your agency’s growth, and technical support is included in the subscription.
