What is travel agency management software?

Commercial management software for a travel agency is a tool that centralizes the entire sales cycle of a travel file: booking, quote, order, invoice, client payment, supplier purchase and accounting. Travelife Manage is an example built specifically for tourism.

Unlike standard invoicing software, it handles industry-specific services (flights, hotels, transfers, tours, visas), connects to GDSs and suppliers via API, and calculates VAT on margin. Each transaction is entered only once and automatically feeds reports and accounting.

How much does Travelife travel agency management software cost?

Travelife travel agency management software starts at 199 $ per month with the Travelife ERP Core plan (5 users included, 1,500 $ setup fee), which covers quotes, orders, customer and supplier invoicing, payments, cash management, margin tracking, CRM, multi-currency and VAT on margin.

The Business (399 $/month, 10 users) and Connect (599 $/month, 20 users) plans add the catalog, hotel contracts, booking engine and GDS / API connections. Travelife takes no commission on your sales. Details on the pricing page.

Does Travelife travel agency management software handle VAT on margin?

Yes. Travelife Manage applies the VAT rules specific to travel agencies and automatically calculates VAT on margin based on the selling price and supplier costs of each booking file.

Invoices are generated directly from the booking engines, with automatic calculation of service fees, and every invoice is recorded immediately in the accounting module. This way you avoid manual calculation errors and secure your tax compliance. Full details on our VAT on margin software for travel agencies page.

Can my external accountant access the software?

Yes. Because Travelife is 100% cloud, your external accountant can view your data online without having to come to your agency, with access limited to the information they need.

With accounts kept up to date in real time, they can quickly identify files that need further checking and close accounting periods without wasting time.

Can I restrict each employee’s access to certain information?

Yes. Access to Travelife Manage is set according to each person's role and the confidentiality level of your agency's information: each user has a profile with their own permissions.

An agent can track their revenue and profits in real time over a given period, while the agency manager views the performance of each agent, or of all their agencies if they run several.
