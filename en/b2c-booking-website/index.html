What is a B2C booking website for travel agencies?

It's a website where your direct customers (individuals, travelers) search, compare and book their own trips without going through an advisor. With Travelife, the agency website includes a B2C booking engine that queries GDSs, your suppliers and your direct contracts to display flights, hotels, trips and activities with their availability. The agency sells 24/7, from anywhere, to millions of tourists and travelers, while bookings flow automatically into its back office.

Can my customers book and pay online 24/7 on my agency website?

Yes. Online payment is part of the Travelife website standard plan. The website connects to secure payment gateways such as CMI in Morocco, and you set your collection rules: full payment at purchase, online deposit with balance management, or a simple quote request. The booking flow is encrypted with HTTPS and the website structure is GDPR-compliant. Every paid booking is saved instantly in your back office, day or night.

How do I set my margins and commissions on website sales?

You keep absolute control over your markups and commissions. The rules you define in Travelife are applied automatically to the rates sent by GDS and suppliers, before they are displayed on your website. The sales process is thus fully automated, and your profitability stays protected. For its part, Travelife takes no commission on the revenue generated through your B2C website: you pay a fixed subscription, which keeps your budget predictable.

Is my travel agency website multilingual and multi-currency?

Yes, from the standard plan. The Travelife website is multilingual and multi-currency, so you can target both local customers and international travelers. It also includes trip search filters (country, city, type, date…), dynamic itinerary mapping on Google Maps, YouTube video integration, social media connection with a sharing module, and a blog section to enrich your content and SEO.

What is the difference between the standard and premium website plans?

The standard plan includes everything you need to sell online: connection to the leading GDSs (1 flight, 1 hotel), a customizable website template, flight, hotel, trip and activity modules, online payment, a back office for booking tracking, native SEO and a 1-year warranty and maintenance. The premium plan adds multiple GDS connections, a custom design, transfer and ferry modules, SMS API integration and dynamic packages. It suits agencies selling a broad, multi-component catalog.

Do website bookings go straight into my back office?

Yes. Every quote request and booking made on the website is automatically saved in the Travelife back-office manager. Your teams no longer need Excel spreadsheets or to re-enter booking files: they track bookings, manage suppliers directly and add customer follow-up notes from a single interface. Back-office access is role-based, so each team member sees the information relevant to them.
