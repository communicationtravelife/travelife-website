The principle

What is the VAT on margin of a travel agency?

VAT on margin is a special scheme that applies, in many countries, to travel agencies and tour operators that sell trips in their own name made up of services bought from third parties.

When an agency sells a package (flights, hotel, transfers, excursions), it does not create most of these services itself: it buys them from airlines, hoteliers or inbound agencies, then resells them to the traveler. The margin scheme starts from this observation: rather than taxing the full price of the trip, it taxes the agency's actual added value, that is, its margin.

In return, the VAT paid on services purchased for the traveler is generally not deductible, and the VAT due does not always appear on the invoice in the same way as under the standard regime. These rules vary from country to country.

The margin scheme also differs from intermediary activity: when the agency acts in the name and on behalf of the client or supplier and receives a commission, other rules apply. A single agency can therefore handle files under different regimes, which makes manual record-keeping tricky.

The pitfalls

Why is calculating VAT on margin manually risky?

On paper, the formula is simple. In an agency handling dozens of files a month, it becomes a source of errors and wasted time.

Multi-service files

One margin, several suppliers

A tour combines tickets, hotels, transfers and guides bought from different suppliers, sometimes on different dates. Forgetting a single purchase invoice skews the file's margin, and therefore the VAT.

Cancellations and credit notes

Files that keep changing

Name change, partial cancellation, supplier penalty, client credit note: every change must be reflected in the margin and the VAT already calculated. In a spreadsheet, these corrections are easily lost.

Currencies

Purchases in foreign currencies

A hotel paid in euros and sold in dirhams: the exchange rate used changes the margin. You need a consistent, traceable rule.

Mixed regimes

Margin or commission?

Ticket-only sales, packages, commission-based services: mixing regimes in a single file leads to inconsistent returns.

Period close

Stressful period ends

Reconciling sales, purchases and collections on the eve of a return ties up the team and delays the accountant's work.

Automation

How does Travelife calculate margin and VAT per file?

In Travelife Manage, VAT on margin is not a separate calculation: it is the last step of a file whose data is already in the ERP.

1

The booking (GDS, hotel API or direct contract) creates a file. The quote, then the client invoice, set the selling price of each service.

2

Linked purchases

Supplier costs are attached to the same file, line by line, with their currency and payment due dates.

3

Margin and VAT calculated

Travelife calculates the margin of each service and of the file, then the VAT according to the rules and rate set up for your agency.

4

Automatic posting

The amounts feed the invoice, the sales and purchase journals and the period's VAT report, with no re-entry.

Any later change (credit note, cancellation, additional purchase) recalculates the file's margin. You keep a record of every operation, which your accountant can view.

What is VAT on margin for a travel agency?

It is a special VAT scheme that applies, in many countries, to travel agencies and tour operators that resell services bought from third parties in their own name. VAT is then charged on the margin made by the agency, not on the total price paid by the traveler.

The conditions of application, the tax base and the rate depend on each country's regulations.

How is the margin of a travel file calculated?

In principle, the margin is the difference between the price invoiced to the client and the cost of the services purchased to deliver the trip (transport, accommodation, transfers, activities...). VAT is then calculated on this margin according to the applicable rules.

The details (margin including or excluding tax, per-file or global calculation, negative margins) vary according to legislation: have your method validated by your chartered accountant.

What is the applicable VAT on margin rate?

The rate depends on the country, the nature of the services and the regulations in force at the time of the transaction. Travelife does not set the rate for you: it is configured in the software during setup, together with your agency and your advisor.

If regulations change, the configuration can be adjusted without changing tools.

Do all of an agency's sales fall under the margin scheme?

No, not necessarily. Services sold as an intermediary, in the name and on behalf of a client or supplier and paid by commission, may be treated differently. A single agency can therefore have files subject to different regimes.

Travelife lets you distinguish these flows in files and reports, which makes the accountant's work easier.

How does Travelife automatically calculate VAT on margin?

Each file groups the sales (quotes, invoices) and the linked supplier purchases. Travelife calculates the margin per service and per file, then the VAT according to the agency's setup, and carries the result over to the invoice, the journals and the period's VAT report.

Credit notes, cancellations and additional purchases automatically recalculate the file's margin.

Does the software handle purchases in foreign currencies?

Yes. Travelife is multi-currency: a supplier purchase can be entered in its original currency and linked to a file sold in another currency. The margin is thus calculated on complete data, without manual conversion in a spreadsheet.

Is Travelife compatible with DGI e-invoicing?

Travelife produces structured invoices from a cloud ERP, not documents re-keyed by hand, which is the foundation of e-invoicing. The solution is maintained and updated by Travelife to keep pace with the reform led by Morocco's Directorate General of Taxes, according to the timeline and requirements set by the authority.

Does the software replace my chartered accountant?

No. Travelife automates the calculation, invoicing and production of journals and reports, which greatly reduces data entry and errors. Your chartered accountant remains your go-to contact to validate the applicable regime, the setup and tax returns.
