Key facts Hajj & Umrah management software

  • ProductTravelife Umrah is software for travel agencies. It is used to prepare, sell and track Umrah departures, with a structured file for each departure.
  • Hybrid modelTravelife Umrah offers three management modes, selectable for each program: 100% offline management (no booking engine), sales catalog and advanced quoting.
  • PackagesTravelife Umrah links each package to the hotels in Makkah and Madinah, the number of nights, the room types (double, triple, quad) and capacities.
  • QuotingTravelife Umrah calculates cost prices, selling prices and forecast margin from your purchase contracts (flights, hotels, services) and your pricing rules.
  • OperationsTravelife Umrah allocates rooms by city and by hotel and prepares the rooming lists and passenger lists for each departure.
  • Travelife suiteTravelife Umrah relies on Admin for offers, Manage for orders and operations, and SmartCRM for customer relationships.

What is Umrah management software for travel agencies?

Umrah management software centralizes the entire cycle of a pilgrimage program: package preparation, sales to customers, participant tracking and departure organization. Travelife Omra brings these steps together in a structured file per departure: hotels in Makkah and Madinah, nights, room types, sales, payments, rooming lists and passenger lists. Sales, operations and management teams work from the same information instead of juggling scattered Excel files. The software integrates with the Travelife suite (Admin, Manage, SmartCRM).

What is the Travelife Omra hybrid model?

The hybrid model combines the flexibility of direct sales and tailor-made programs with the rigor of structured file-based management. Your teams create flexible packages while keeping centralized tracking that is simple and identical for every departure.

How does offline file management work?

Your team organizes each departure within a single file. You record proposals, track customer sales, link participants and centralize operational items. The result: fewer scattered files and information that is instantly available to sales and operations teams.

Do I need an online booking engine to use Travelife Omra?

No. The “100% offline management” mode lets you prepare the file directly and enter your sales without going through a booking engine. It's the simplest starting point for an agency selling its Umrah packages over the counter, by phone or through its networks. Whenever you're ready, you can structure a sales catalog to present your departures, packages and rooms in a booking engine, then move on to advanced costing. All three modes share the same sales and operations tracking.

Is Travelife Omra suitable for both reseller agencies and program organizers?

Yes. Travelife Omra is designed both for reseller agencies, which sell existing programs, and for agencies that build their own Umrah programs. The former mainly rely on offline management and the sales catalog; the latter also use advanced costing, which calculates cost prices and rates from their purchase contracts. In both cases, each departure keeps its participants, rooms and operational details in a file shared by the whole team.

How are offers between Makkah and Madinah prepared?

The Admin module lets you link your hotels, overnight stays, room types (double, triple, quad) and accommodation capacities to each package. Your teams thus have a common reference to sell the offer and prepare its operations with peace of mind.

How is the online sales catalog structured?

The catalog gathers all your programs and packages ready for sale. It feeds your booking engine directly, and your customers read your offers more easily.

How does advanced costing calculate cost prices?

Quoting centralizes your purchase contracts (flights, hotels, ancillary services). The rules you have configured automate calculations by package, room occupancy and departure settings. If your purchase prices change, the quote updates instantly according to your rules: you keep full control of your prices.

What is the difference between projected margin and actual result?

The forecast margin is calculated before the sale, when simulating prices (quoting). The result, in turn, is based on the sales, payments and purchases actually recorded in the file. You compare the two directly from the sales and purchase journals and the operations sheet.

How do I track orders, payments and invoicing?

Everything is tracked from the sales module. From the customer invoice to the departure's financial summary, you track payments, invoicing and collections in real time.

How are rooming lists and on-site logistics managed?

The operations module organizes participants and rooms by departure, by city and by hotel. It automatically generates the rooming lists and passenger lists essential to a smooth stay.

How does Travelife Omra fit into our agency management?

Travelife Umrah relies on the entire Travelife suite: offer preparation in Admin, orders and operational management in Manage, and customer relationship tracking in SmartCRM, depending on your setup.
