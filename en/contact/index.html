How does Travelife support the digital transformation of travel agencies?

Travelife is a 100% cloud ERP designed to digitalize and modernize every operation of a travel agency.

The platform connects to suppliers (APIs & GDS), brings your B2B & B2C sales together in one place and automates your sales and financial tasks. You track your agency's results in real time.

Beyond the software, Travelife supports agencies with strategy consulting, digital marketing and communication consulting.

How does Travelife improve my travel agency's profitability?

Travelife centralizes your sales, margins and invoicing in an ERP built for tourism.

B2B & B2C bookings are recorded automatically: fewer errors and better payment tracking.

Dashboards and detailed reports show you your most profitable products, so you can decide where to focus your efforts.

Does Travelife integrate with GDSs and international suppliers?

Yes. Travelife connects to the leading GDSs and international suppliers through secure APIs.

You access thousands of flights, hotels and tourism services from a single screen, and bookings update automatically.

With multiple suppliers connected, you offer a wider range and adjust your margins in real time.

Does Travelife offer a B2C website connected to the ERP?

Yes. Travelife offers a B2C website fully synchronized with the ERP.

Availability, rates and bookings flow automatically into your back office, with no double entry.

You sell online 24/7 while keeping full control of your operations.

How long does it take to deploy Travelife?

Travelife runs in the cloud: nothing to install, so deployment is fast.

Depending on your needs (ERP, B2B, B2C, API & GDS), setup can be completed in a few days to a few weeks.

The Travelife team handles the setup, data import and training. Your agency keeps working normally during the transition.

How can I get a personalized demo?

To get a personalized demo of Travelife, simply fill in the contact form on this page.

Our team studies your business, the size of your travel agency and your needs, then prepares a presentation tailored to your organization.

During the demo, we show you how our cloud ERP brings your bookings together, automates your invoicing, manages your CRM and connects your B2B and B2C sales.

You will also discover our modules for sales management, reporting and API integration with suppliers and GDSs.

The demo is completely free and with no commitment. You see first-hand how Travelife can simplify the management of your travel agency and improve its profitability.

Can I run a Hajj & Umrah agency with Travelife?

Yes. Travelife handles your Hajj & Umrah groups end to end: allotments (rooms and seats reserved in advance), installment payments, custom packages and detailed booking file tracking.

Built-in reports show you the profitability of each departure.

How much does Travelife travel agency software cost?

Travelife travel agency software costs from 199 € to 599 € per month depending on the plan, plus a one-time setup fee. The Travelife ERP Core plan (ERP only, 5 users included) is 199 € per month, with a 1,500 € setup fee.

The ERP Business plan (ERP + offer and catalog administration console, 10 users) is 399 € per month, with a 3,000 € setup fee. The ERP Connect plan (ERP + API integrations, 20 users, 2 APIs included) is 599 € per month, with a 4,000 € setup fee.

Travelife takes no commission on your sales, and you can change plans at any time. Full details are available on the Travelife software pricing page.

Can Travelife software be funded by the GO SIYAHA program?

Yes. Travelife is an approved Maroc PME provider under the GO SIYAHA program, which supports the digital transformation of travel agencies in Morocco and can fund up to 90% of the project.

Travelife solutions (sales management ERP, B2C website, B2B platform, Hajj & Umrah software) can qualify under this program.

During your free demo, our team presents your project's implementation plan and the steps to obtain GO SIYAHA funding.

Is Travelife suitable for a small travel agency?

Yes. Travelife is designed to support agencies of all sizes, from independent agencies to agency networks.

A small agency can start with the Travelife ERP Core plan at €199 per month (5 users included), which covers quotes, orders, customer and supplier invoicing, payment and cash management, margin tracking, CRM and VAT on margin, without APIs or a booking engine.

Since you can change plans at any time, the agency can later add a B2C website, API connections or more users as it grows, without switching software.
