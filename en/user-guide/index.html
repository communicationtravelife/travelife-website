Key performance indicators (KPIs)

Advanced financial reporting

Revenue Y vs Y-1

Track the evolution of your revenue accurately. Travelife automatically sorts your revenue by sales channel and compares it with the previous year (N vs N-1).

  • Overall sales revenue: by year, by month and by day.
  • Breakdown by customer: B2B (corporate accounts) or B2C (individual travelers).
  • Invoicing tracking: comparison between revenue achieved and revenue actually collected.

Business workflow monitoring

Productivity

Stay in control of your team's activity. The dashboard displays important tasks so that no lead is forgotten.

  • Pending proposals: number of quotes sent that are still waiting for the customer's reply.
  • Rankings: top 5 agents (most productive) and top 5 customers (highest revenue).
  • Alerts: urgent tasks and booking file deadlines.

Cash management

Cash flow and collections

Outstanding receivables & payments

Know at any time how much money is coming into and going out of the agency. Travelife immediately spots missing payments and customers at risk of not paying.

  • Tracking of the amounts your customers owe you (outstanding receivables) and your supplier debts.
  • Quick validation of payments received.
  • Charts of upcoming revenue, calculated from confirmed booking files.

Product mix analysis

Margin by department

Spot the products that earn you the most. Travelife breaks down your profits by department: airline ticketing, accommodation, tour packages, visas and transport services.

  • View by sales department.
  • Identification of the most profitable products.
02Own product

Bookings & inventory

Access: Main menu › Booking

The Travelife booking engine handles two sources at the same time: your direct contracts (your internal inventory) and offers from around the world, received automatically from your suppliers via XML / API. You can sell everything your customers need, from the hotel to the airline ticket issued on the GDS.

Accommodation & flights

Accommodation management

Bookings > Hotel

Sell your exclusive negotiated rates alongside hotels from the world's leading suppliers. Travelife updates availability and prices automatically, so your offer stays competitive.

  • Internal hotels: enter your direct contracts, allotments (rooms reserved in advance), stop sales (sales closures) and seasonal rates.
  • Hotel API: direct access to global bedbanks (room wholesalers), with instant confirmation.

Air ticketing

Bookings > Flights (API)

Travelife is connected directly to airline reservation systems. You manage your PNRs (air booking records) effortlessly, with real-time prices and automated ticketing.

  • Multi-destination search and airline filter.
  • Ticket issuing via API and tracking of issued tickets.

Travel production & services

Destination logistics

Bookings > Activities / Transfers

Manage your transport and on-site activities. Transfers can be connected via API to external providers so you can see their availability in real time.

  • API connection for international transfers.
  • Activity and excursion management in offline mode (manual entry).

Tours & excursions management

Bookings > Tours

Create and manage your tours and group excursions through manual (offline) entry: itinerary, included services and number of seats.

Package tours

Bookings > Packages

Manage your packages through manual (offline) entry: group tours, Umrah and other combined offers.

Visa sales management

Bookings > Visa

Manage your visa applications through manual (offline) entry, with a set fee for each destination.

03Customer relations

CRM & Customer Experience

Access: Main menu › CRM

Travelife's CRM (customer relationship management) module brings together everything you know about your clients. Designed for travel agencies, it keeps all your exchanges with partners (B2B) and travelers (B2C) in one place. With this single database, you can segment clients by profile, track sales results and find any exchange in seconds.

Third-party management & data integrity

360° customer & traveler profile

CRM > Customers > Customer list

Keep your travelers' full history to offer truly personalized service. Travelife gathers every purchase, every preference and every official document on a single record.

  • Classification: sort your accounts by type (company, individual).
  • Traveler details: passports, loyalty programs and dietary preferences.
  • Financial position: sales history, payments received and balance due, updated in real time.

Supplier & partner profile

CRM > Suppliers > Supplier list

Just like your clients, each supplier (hotel, carrier, guide) has a complete record to track your relationship with them.

  • History: all bookings made, cancelled or modified with this provider.
  • Accounting follow-up: purchase invoices, payments sent and supplier balance, available instantly.

Traceability & sales workflow

Communication history

CRM > Communication Log

The whole team can see what was sent to each client. Every outgoing message is logged, which protects you in the event of a commercial or legal dispute.

  • Email archiving: proposals, quotes and confirmations sent from the ERP.
  • Who sent what: the agent's name and the date of each send.
  • Attachments: find the exact version of the proposal or invoice sent to the client.

Pipelines & conversion

CRM > Sales pipelines

Track every sale, from the client's first inquiry to confirmation. Spot the most promising deals and see how long your sales take.

  • Lead stages: visual tracking of stages (qualification, proposal, negotiation).

Support & data quality

Ticket management & after-sales service

CRM > Tasks & Tickets

Organize your customer service and complaints. With tickets, you prioritize each special request and respond to your clients faster.

Database hygiene

CRM > Customers > Duplicate management

Travelife automatically detects duplicate client records. You can safely merge the two records without losing their financial history or booking files.

04Sales

Sales & sales administration

Access: Main menu › Sales / Commercial

The Sales / Commercial module turns your inquiries into sales. It covers the entire process: you build a tailor-made offer, the client approves it, then you place orders with suppliers. Bookings received via API and those entered manually are brought together in one place, so you stay in control of your entire catalog.

Offer design & proposals

Creating quotes & tour proposals

Sales > Proposals > New quote

Build tailor-made offers by combining several services. You enter each price line manually and adapt it exactly to the client's request.

  • New quote: quickly create a personalized offer.
  • Quote list: find proposals awaiting a reply so you can follow up with your clients.
  • Tour proposal list: manage complex itineraries and group tours separately.

Quotation & cost calculation

Sales > Quotation

For group trips or complex projects, calculate your costs and margin before sending the quote.

  • New quotation: simulate the costs and gross margin of a booking file.
  • Quotation list: find all the price calculations already done for your clients.

Sales & order management

Customer order cycle

Sales > Sales

When the client accepts the offer, the quote becomes a firm order and a booking file.

  • New order: record the firm sale; the file reference is created automatically.
  • Order list: see the status of each of the agency's sales.
  • Sales report: analyze your sales by period, by agent or by product.

Supplier orders & POs

Sales > Purchases

Manage your orders with suppliers. Travelife creates purchase orders (POs) that confirm and secure each service.

  • Order list: all your ongoing purchases from providers.
  • Purchase report: analyze your cost prices to monitor your profitability.

Book Offline & manual inventory

Offline bookings

Sales > Book Offline

Some services are not connected via API (local guides, restaurants, small hotels). This module lets you enter them manually and add them to the client's file.

  • New booking: manual entry of a service (flight, accommodation, transfer, activity, boat, extra).
  • Booking list: summary of all manually entered services, for operations follow-up.

Inventory & service list

Sales > Bookings

View all booked services, whether they come from an automatic connection or manual entry.

  • Ticket / flight list: tracking of all airline tickets issued.
  • Hotel list (internal & API): tracking of confirmed room nights.
  • Package list: all packages sold, in one place.
05Finance

Financial management & Accounting / Cash

Access: Main menu › Accounting / Cash

The Accounting / Cash module of Travelife ERP manages all of the agency's finances, following the accounting rules specific to travel agencies. Every sale feeds directly into the accounts: you track money received from clients, money paid to suppliers and your tax obligations (VAT, VAT on margin, credit reports).

Invoicing management (customers & suppliers)

Invoicing & collection cycle

Accounting / Cash > Customers

Invoice and collect payment faster thanks to automated invoicing and payments. You see clearly what each client still owes you.

  • Invoicing: individual invoices, grouped invoices and credit notes.
  • Payments: one payment for one invoice, or one payment for several invoices.
  • Follow-up: detailed financial statement and list of files not yet invoiced.

Purchases & payables management

Accounting / Cash > Suppliers

Compare each supplier invoice with the bookings actually made. You keep your costs under control and never overpay a provider.

  • Entry: recording of supplier invoices and purchase credit notes.
  • Payments: tracking of payments sent and history of bank transactions.
  • Forecast: financial statement by supplier to anticipate due dates and your cash flow needs.

Treasury & cash flow

Liquidity monitoring

Accounting / Cash > Cash registers

See in real time the cash available at each point of sale and in each bank account. Every cent is traced.

  • Cash report: expected balance and actual balance of your cash desks, in one click.
  • Transactions: complete journal of inflows (sales) and outflows (expenses / purchases).
  • Bank reconciliation: check that your bank statements match the ERP.

Analytics & balances

Accounting / Cash > Accounting

An overview of the agency's financial health: instantly see who owes you money and how much you owe your partners.

  • Balances: list of amounts owed by your clients and amounts you owe your suppliers.
  • Payment methods: taxes and fees applied depending on the collection method (card terminal, bank transfer, etc.).

Taxation & DGI compliance

VAT control

Accounting / Cash > Accounting > VAT

Prepare your tax returns in a few clicks with regulation-compliant reports (DGI). Travelife automatically separates VAT-liable transactions from VAT-exempt ones.

  • VAT control: check the VAT collected on sales against payments received.
  • Supplier VAT control: track recoverable VAT on your purchase invoices.

Credit & risk management

Accounting / Cash > Accounting > Credit reports

Avoid unpaid invoices with credit reports. These reports alert you when a client on account exceeds the credit limit you have granted them.

  • Client credit report: risk per client and average payment time.
  • Supplier credit report: credit extended to you by your main partners.
06Operations

Booking file & project management

Access: Main menu › Files

The Files module is used to manage your group trips (GIT), tours and corporate events (MICE). On a single dashboard, it brings together the sales made to each traveler and the purchases made for the whole group. This way you know precisely what each file earns.

Operational management & yield

Load factor tracking (pax)

Progress indicators

Instantly see how many seats are sold on each departure, thanks to gauges updated in real time.

  • Seats sold: number of seats sold out of the total available (e.g., 3 / 17 seats).
  • Occupancy rate: percentage calculated automatically, so you know whether to step up your marketing.
  • Status: files "In progress", "Confirmed" or "Closed".

Financial performance

Actual and forecast margins

Track precisely what each project earns: the margin already achieved and the forecast margin.

  • Sales (revenue): total of client orders linked to the project.
  • Gross margin & markup rate: profit and markup of the file, calculated in real time.
  • Uninvoiced purchases: alert when a supplier has not yet sent its invoice for an ordered service.

Consolidated flows (sales & purchases)

Booking file sales journal

Customer tracking

Find all of a project's client orders, invoiced or not, for seamless client follow-up.

  • Traceability: complete list of order numbers (CO) and linked invoices (FA).
  • Detail by client: travelers, amount paid by each and invoicing status.

Purchase journal & group purchases

Cost price control

Keep your costs under control by recording the services purchased for the whole group.

  • Add a group purchase: enter in one go a supplier invoice (accommodation, transport, guide) that applies to the whole file.
  • PO control: check that purchase orders match the services booked and the invoices received.
07Operations

Logistics planning & coordination

Access: Main menu › Planning

The Planning module is your agency's calendar. It displays all booked services day by day: hotels, flights, transfers, activities. Your teams know who is arriving, who is leaving and what to organize, without forgetting anything.

Calendar views & monitoring

Dynamic accommodation calendar

Planning > Hotels

Track your hotel bookings day by day. The planning clearly shows where each booking comes from, so you can react faster.

  • Contract hotel: room nights booked under your own directly negotiated contracts.
  • Hotel API: bookings confirmed by connected partner platforms (XML feeds).
  • Book Offline: bookings entered manually with providers that are not connected.
  • Capacity: number of rooms and bookings per hotel.

Movement coordination

Planning > Activities & Flights

Organize transport and activities so that no traveler is left without assistance.

  • Activities: schedule of excursions, transfers and on-site services, to best allocate guides and drivers.
  • Flights: arrivals and departures, essential for organizing airport greeting and shuttles.

Operational benefits

Logistics planning ahead

Weekly view

With the weekly view, your agents spot busy periods in advance and check available rooms at a glance.

Centralized sources

A single calendar

Travelife shows bookings from all sources (API, manual entry, stock contracts) on a single calendar: no service is overlooked.

How do I create a quote in Travelife ERP?

To create a quote in Travelife, open the Sales / Commercial menu > Proposals > New quote. This screen lets you build a tailor-made offer by combining several services and adapting each price line to the client's request.

You then track sent quotes in the Quote list, to follow up with clients who have not yet replied. For a group trip or complex project, first use Sales / Commercial > Quotation to simulate costs and gross margin before issuing the quote. Once accepted, the quote becomes an order in Sales / Commercial > Sales.

How do I track the margin on a group trip in Travelife?

Group trip margins are tracked in the Booking Files module (Main Menu > Booking files), designed for group travel (GIT), tours and MICE events.

Each file displays in real time the revenue from linked client orders, gross margin, markup rate and occupancy rate (for example 3 / 17 seats). Group purchases (accommodation, transport, guide) are entered in the file's purchase journal. An alert notifies you when a supplier has not yet sent its invoice for an ordered service. This way you know the real profitability of each departure.

How do I control VAT with Travelife software?

VAT control is done in Accounting / Cash > Accounting > VAT. Travelife produces reports compliant with current regulations (DGI compliance) and separates VAT-liable transactions from VAT-exempt ones. For agency-specific rules, see our VAT on margin page.

The VAT Control report checks VAT collected on sales against payments received, and Supplier VAT Control tracks recoverable VAT on purchase invoices. These reports make it easier to prepare your tax returns. Customer and supplier credit reports, in the same Accounting menu, round out the agency's financial management.

Can Travelife handle services that are not connected via API?

Yes. The Sales > Book Offline module lets you manually add services not connected via API, such as local guides, restaurants or small hotels.

This lets you enter flights, accommodations, transfers, activities, boats or extras directly in the client's overall file. These manually entered bookings then appear in the booking list and in the Planning, alongside API bookings and direct hotel contracts. Travelife thus centralizes all sources on a single calendar, so no service is overlooked.

Is Travelife ERP training provided at onboarding?

Yes. As soon as your license is activated, Travelife experts run a full training session for your teams, so they master the operational workflow from A to Z, from creating a customer to closing the books.

After training, this user guide serves as a module-by-module reference. For any technical question, the support team answers by email at support@travelife.ma, with remote assistance if needed, and a dedicated WhatsApp group lets you chat directly with the technical team according to your maintenance pack.
