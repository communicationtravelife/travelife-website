Who we are

Who is Travelife, the travel agency software company?

Travelife is a Moroccan technology company focused on a single industry: travel agencies.

Since it was founded in Casablanca in 2013, Travelife has been designing, developing and continuously improving software for travel agencies, tour operators, DMCs (inbound agencies), agency networks and OTAs. Where a generic ERP has to be adapted to tourism, Travelife natively handles the realities of the trade: multi-service booking files, GDS connectivity, vouchers and rooming lists, commissions, deposits and automatic calculation of VAT on margin.

Travelife is both a software publisher and a partner: our teams do more than deliver a license, they support every agency from the initial audit through to post-launch follow-up.

  • Designed and developed in Morocco
  • No commission on sales
  • Dedicated support team

What is Travelife?

Travelife is a Moroccan travel agency software company, founded in 2013 in Casablanca. It offers a 100% cloud (SaaS) platform that brings together a travel agency management ERP, a B2C website, a B2B booking platform, a SmartCRM with a WhatsApp AI Agent and an Umrah and Hajj solution.

Where is Travelife’s headquarters located?

Travelife’s headquarters are located at Office No. 6, Office Panorama, 14 avenue Abou Bakr el Kadiri, Sidi Maarouf, Casablanca (Morocco). The team can be reached at (+212) 5 21 50 06 05 or at contact@travelife.ma.

How many travel agencies use Travelife?

More than 200 travel agencies use Travelife in Morocco and worldwide: agencies, tour operators, DMCs and Hajj and Umrah specialists. Discover our clients.

What software does Travelife offer?

Travelife offers Travelife Manage (travel agency management software and ERP), a B2C travel agency website, a B2B booking system, SmartCRM and the AI Agent and Travelife Omra.

What awards has Travelife won?

Is Travelife listed with Maroc PME?

Yes. Travelife is listed with Maroc PME under the GO SIYAHA program. Moroccan travel agencies can therefore fund up to 90% of their digital transformation: management software, B2C website, B2B platform, digital marketing and SmartCRM.

Which GDSs and suppliers is Travelife connected to?

Travelife is connected to the Amadeus, Sabre and Galileo Travelport GDSs, to aggregators such as Travelfusion, Misterfly and FlyConnect, to hotel bed banks such as Hotelbeds, WebBeds and Restel, and to payment solutions such as CMI, Payzone and PayPal.

In total, more than 1,500 airlines and 500,000 accommodations are available. See our partners.

How much does Travelife software cost?

Travelife’s management software costs 199 €, 399 € or 599 € per month depending on the plan (ERP Core, Business or Connect), and B2C websites start at 99 € per month. Travelife takes no commission on sales. See pricing.
