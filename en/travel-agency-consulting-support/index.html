Why support matters

Why is expert support key to digitizing a travel agency?

Most software projects that fail in agencies don’t stumble on technology, but on organization: poorly migrated data, under-trained teams, processes that don’t match the business.

Multi-service booking files, suppliers, deposits, commissions, VAT on margin: reproducing an agency’s reality in a tool requires real industry knowledge. Travelife support translates the way you work into the software, then helps your teams adopt it.

  • A contact who speaks the language of travel agencies.
  • A start on clean data, with teams becoming autonomous faster.

What does Travelife's travel agency onboarding involve?

Travelife onboarding covers the agency's entire digitalization project: business audit, module recommendation, software setup, migration of existing data, team training, supported launch, then support and follow-up after go-live.

The goal is for the software to truly match the way you work and for your teams to use it every day.

What are the steps of an onboarding project?

A project follows 7 steps: 1) agency audit, 2) module recommendation, 3) setup, 4) data migration, 5) team training, 6) launch, 7) follow-up and growth.

Does Travelife import data from my old software or my Excel files?

Yes. Data migration is part of our onboarding: your customer, supplier, product and contract files, whether from spreadsheets or a previous tool, are checked with you and then imported into Travelife.

How are my teams trained?

As soon as your license is activated, Travelife experts run a complete training session to master the operational workflow from A to Z, from customer creation to accounting close.

Your teams then have access to the Travelife Academy videos on the Manage software and to the ERP user guide.

What is the Travelife Academy?

The Travelife Academy is a series of training videos, launched in 2024, designed to help users master the Manage commercial management software.

It lets you train a new agent at their own pace or review a specific procedure, as a complement to the initial training.

How does support work after launch?

Every customer agency has access to the support desk at contact@travelife.ma, with remote assistance if needed.

Technical assistance is included in the subscription, and software updates are deployed automatically.

How much does Travelife onboarding cost?

The Travelife software subscription comes in three plans: ERP Core at €199 / month, ERP Business at €399 / month and ERP Connect at €599 / month.
