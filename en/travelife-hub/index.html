Our vision

Travelife HUB: connecting supply, distribution and operations

Travel professionals juggle suppliers, sales channels and tools that do not talk to each other. Travelife HUB is our vision of an ecosystem that links travel supply, its distribution and its operational delivery.

Book a demo See our solutions
In short

Travelife HUB is neither a simple API catalogue nor a new name for our products. It is what ties our solutions together: connecting supplier content, making it easier to distribute in B2B and B2C, and carrying every sale through into the agency’s day-to-day management. Part of this vision is available today; the rest is being built step by step.

What Travelife HUB connects

Five links, one chain

Suppliers and product sources

Flights, hotels, transfers, activities and direct contracts, through API connections, GDS or manual entry depending on each project.

Distributors and agencies

The businesses that resell travel products to their professional network, with their own commercial terms.

B2B and B2C channels

Booking platforms for partners and online sales websites for travellers.

Customer relationships

Incoming requests, leads, opportunities and follow-ups tracked in SmartCRM.

Bookings and operations

Files, invoicing, payments and activity tracking in Travelife Manage.

From fragmented tools to a connected business

When tools are separate

  • the same booking is re-entered in several systems;
  • a request received in the evening waits until the next day;
  • supplier offers never reach resellers;
  • the real margin only shows up at the end of the month.

When the business is connected

  • information entered once flows between sales, bookings and management;
  • every request is tracked until a sales agent takes it over;
  • products are distributed on the B2B and B2C channels that suit you;
  • the business is managed from up-to-date data.

Agentic AI

AI agents that support your teams

Agentic AI is artificial intelligence that can use tools and carry out certain authorised steps in a business process: understanding a request, searching a catalogue, collecting the information needed, then passing on a structured file. Your teams stay in control: permissions, approvals and oversight of actions remain human.

Frequently asked questions

What is Travelife HUB?

Travelife HUB is Travelife’s ecosystem vision: connecting supplier content, its B2B and B2C distribution, customer relationships and the operations of travel businesses.

Does Travelife HUB replace Travelife’s products?

No. Travelife Manage, SmartCRM, the B2B platforms and the B2C websites keep their names and roles. Travelife HUB brings them together and describes how they connect.

Is everything already available?

No. Part of it is available today, other features are being rolled out progressively and some are part of our longer-term ambition. Active supplier connections depend on each project’s contracts and configuration.

What role does artificial intelligence play?

AI is used for concrete tasks: qualifying a request, helping prepare a proposal, passing a file to SmartCRM or supporting follow-ups. Actions remain governed by permissions and human approval.
