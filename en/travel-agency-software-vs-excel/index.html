Excel’s limits

What Excel really costs an agency

Double entry

The same client, ticket and invoice are copied into several files. Every copy is a chance to make a mistake.

VAT on margin errors

One broken formula or forgotten line, and the declared VAT is wrong. A tax audit does not make mistakes.

No view of your margin

How much does each file, each agent, each month really earn? With Excel, the answer comes too late, if it comes at all.

Fragile data

A broken computer, an overwritten file, an employee leaving with a copy: the agency’s whole history is at risk.

When Excel is still enough, and when to switch

Excel may be enough if…

  • you handle only a few files per month;
  • you work alone, without a team or partners;
  • you do not sell online.

It is time to switch to software if…

  • you spend more time entering data than selling;
  • several people edit the same files;
  • you do not know your real margin before the end of the month;
  • you want to sell online or to partner agencies;
  • you need to prepare for e-invoicing in 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Is travel agency software really more cost-effective than Excel?

Excel is free, but it wastes time on every file: repeated data entry, VAT on margin calculations, reminders, exports for the accountant. Software like Travelife automates these tasks and shows your margin in real time. For most agencies, the time saved and the errors avoided quickly outweigh the subscription price.

Is my data safer in Travelife than in Excel?

Yes. An Excel file can be copied, overwritten or lost with a computer. In Travelife, data is hosted in the cloud, each user has their own account and two-factor authentication protects access.

Can I bring my Excel files into Travelife?

Yes. Data migration is part of our onboarding: your client and supplier files and useful data are imported so your team starts with its history.

Can Excel calculate VAT on margin?

Yes, with formulas, but everything relies on entering each file by hand. One formula error or forgotten line is enough to make the declaration wrong. Travelife calculates VAT on margin automatically on every invoice.

Is Excel compatible with e-invoicing in Morocco?

No. The e-invoicing required by the DGI calls for structured invoices sent in a specific format, which a spreadsheet cannot do. Travelife is already compliant with these standards.
