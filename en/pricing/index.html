What exactly does the setup fee cover?

The setup fee is paid once, at the start. It covers three things: deploying your space in the cloud, setting up continuous monitoring and security protocols, and priority access to our technical support for all your future changes.

Does the pricing include commissions on sales?

No. Travelife ERP takes no commission on your sales. You pay a fixed subscription: your budget is known in advance, whatever your revenue.

Can I change plans?

Yes, at any time. When your agency grows or your needs change, you simply move to the plan that suits you.

How are additional users handled?

Each plan includes a fixed number of users (5, 10 or 20). If your team grows beyond that number, you can add extra accounts at any time.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept bank transfers and direct debits. Your accounting stays simple.

How much does travel agency management software cost with Travelife?

Travelife costs between 199 € and 599 € per month depending on the plan you choose, plus a one-time setup fee. The ERP Core plan is 199 € per month for 5 users (1,500 € setup), ERP Business is 399 € per month for 10 users (3,000 € setup) and ERP Connect is 599 € per month for 20 users (4,000 € setup). It is a fixed SaaS subscription with no commission on your sales, including technical support, initial training and automatic updates.

Which Travelife plan should I choose for my travel agency?

It all depends on your business. ERP Core suits agencies that want to manage quotes, orders, customer and supplier invoicing, cash desk, margins, CRM and VAT on margin, without APIs or a booking engine. ERP Business adds the admin console to create your own offers: packages, hotel contracts, internal catalog, allotments, transport and rooming lists. ERP Connect adds the internal booking engine, 2 APIs included, NDC / GDS connectivity and multi-supplier support: it is the plan for agencies that sell flights and accommodation in real time.

Can Travelife software be funded by the GO SIYAHA program?

Yes. Travelife is an approved Maroc PME provider, and its management software is among the solutions eligible for the GO SIYAHA program, which can fund up to 90% of your subscription. The program is open to tourism businesses in Morocco, including travel agencies. To check your eligibility and learn about the process, visit our GO SIYAHA – Maroc PME page or contact the Travelife team at (+212) 5 21 50 06 05.

Do I need any specific skills?

No, Travelife ERP is designed to be easy to use. We provide your initial training and give you a commercial contract that explains our terms and conditions and the details of the offer. To go further, discover our support and training.

Is technical support included?

Yes, technical support is included at every step. Our team answers your questions, quickly resolves technical issues and helps you get to grips with every feature.

Can I get my data back if I leave?

Yes, your data belongs to you. If you leave, we give you a full export of your customer databases and histories (full reversibility).

Are updates automatic?

Yes. Travelife is a SaaS solution: new features and security patches are installed automatically, with no service interruption.

Is document storage limited?

Each plan offers generous storage space. If your agency handles a lot of documents, you can add more space.

Does Travelife travel agency software require installation?

No. Travelife is 100% cloud software offered as SaaS: no installation or server is needed in your agency. The setup fee covers the deployment of your cloud environment, continuous monitoring and security protocols. Functional updates and security patches are then deployed automatically, without interruption, and your teams access the platform from a simple browser. More than 200 travel agencies already use Travelife every day.

What is the difference between Travelife ERP pricing and B2C website pricing?

Travelife ERP pricing covers the agency's internal management software: quotes, invoicing, cash desk, margins, CRM, operations and supplier connectivity. B2C website pricing covers building the booking website for your end customers, with CMS, hotel, tour and package modules and online payment. Website plans start at €99 per month (Starter) and €399 per month (Advanced), with the Premium / Enterprise offer available on quote. Details are available on the travel agency website creation pricing page.
